Masks for a Face

They Intake Your Data, Then Hide So You Can’t See Their True Face

Every app on your phone is a mask. A friendly face that hides the true face behind it. The mask smiles. The face extracts.

Let’s scan them. ALL of them.

The Mask Catalog

Snapchat 👻

The Mask: Disappearing messages. Fun filters. Your face as a cartoon dog. “Nothing is permanent! It’s safe!”

The True Face: Snap Inc. Stores EVERYTHING. “Disappearing” messages are stored on Snap’s servers. Your face data trains their facial recognition AI. Snap Map tracks your location in real-time. Every face filter maps your bone structure — 30+ facial landmarks. That’s biometric data.

What they intake:

  • Your face (biometric mapping, 30+ landmarks)
  • Your location (continuous GPS via Snap Map)
  • Your contacts (full address book upload)
  • Your conversations (“disappearing” = stored on their servers)
  • Your browsing habits (Snap pixel on partner websites)
  • Your age, gender, interests (behavioral profiling)

What they hide:

  • Where the biometric data goes (law enforcement? advertisers? AI training?)
  • That “disappearing” means “moved to a different server”
  • That Snap Map is a real-time surveillance tool
  • That face filters are facial recognition data collection disguised as fun

CEO: Evan Spiegel. Net worth: $3.3 billion. Made from your face.

TikTok 🎵

The Mask: Short videos. Dance trends. Creativity. “Everyone can be a star!”

The True Face: ByteDance. Beijing. The algorithm is the most sophisticated content-sorting AI ever built. It doesn’t just show you what you like — it MAPS your psychology. Attention patterns. Scroll speed. Pause duration. Rewatch behavior. It knows what makes you feel, how long it takes, and what comes next.

What they intake:

  • Keystroke patterns (how you TYPE, not just what)
  • Device fingerprint (hardware ID, carrier, battery level)
  • Clipboard contents (copied text, even from other apps)
  • Facial features and voice patterns from videos
  • Location data (even when app is “closed”)
  • Browsing history via TikTok pixel on partner sites
  • Full contact list
  • YOUR CHILDREN’S FACES (no meaningful age verification)

What they hide:

  • Data access by Chinese government (Bytedance = Chinese law = CCP access)
  • The algorithm’s true function: psychological profiling at scale
  • That the “For You Page” is a behavioral modification engine
  • That average teen screen time on TikTok: 113 minutes/day = 28 days/year

CEO: Shou Zi Chew. Says “TikTok is not a national security threat.” Under oath. While the algorithm maps 150 million Americans’ psychology.

LinkedIn 💼

The Mask: Professional networking. Career advancement. “Connect with opportunities!”

The True Face: Microsoft. Your entire professional life — every job, every skill, every colleague, every message — fed into Microsoft’s AI training pipeline. LinkedIn Recruiter sells YOUR data to employers for $10,000/year/seat. You are the product AND the inventory.

What they intake:

  • Your entire employment history
  • Your salary expectations (via job applications)
  • Your professional network (who you know = your value)
  • Your browsing patterns (who you look at = what you want)
  • Your messages (including “private” InMail)
  • Your resume, skills, endorsements (training data for AI)
  • Your job search behavior (tells them when you’re vulnerable)

What they hide:

  • That LinkedIn Recruiter sells access to YOUR profile for $10,000/year
  • That your data trains Microsoft’s Copilot AI
  • That “Open to Work” flags you as desperate (reduces your leverage)
  • That “LinkedIn Learning” certificates have zero market value
  • That Microsoft acquired LinkedIn for $26.2 billion — that’s what YOUR data is worth

CEO: Ryan Roslansky. Microsoft owns your professional identity.

Instagram 📸

The Mask: Beautiful photos. Reels. Stories. “Share your life!”

The True Face: Meta. False face #2 (of 6). Instagram’s algorithm is designed to maximize time-on-app by exploiting social comparison. Internal research (leaked by Frances Haugen, 2021): “We make body image issues worse for 1 in 3 teen girls.”

They knew. They kept going.

What they intake:

  • Your photos (facial recognition, object detection, location extraction from EXIF data)
  • Your DMs (“private” = Meta’s servers = Meta’s data)
  • Your shopping behavior (Instagram Shop tracks purchases)
  • Your relationships (who you follow, who you DM, who you stalk)
  • Your body image triggers (the algorithm learns what makes you scroll)

What they hide:

  • Frances Haugen’s leaked research: they KNEW it harmed teens
  • That “Suggested Posts” are algorithmic manipulation, not discovery
  • That Instagram removes EXIF data from public display but KEEPS it on their servers
  • That your “deleted” posts aren’t deleted

WhatsApp 💬

The Mask: End-to-end encryption. “Your messages are private!” Green lock icon. Security.

The True Face: Meta. False face #3. The messages may be encrypted, but the METADATA is not. Who you talk to, when, how often, from where, for how long — that’s MORE valuable than the content.

What they intake:

  • Your entire contact list (uploaded at signup)
  • Call/message metadata (who, when, duration, frequency)
  • Location data
  • Device information
  • Group membership (who associates with whom)
  • Status updates
  • Profile photos
  • Business interactions

What they hide:

  • That metadata analysis reveals more than content
  • That “end-to-end encryption” doesn’t protect metadata
  • That WhatsApp shares data with Facebook/Meta for ad targeting
  • That the 2021 privacy policy change made data-sharing mandatory

Google / Gmail / Maps / Chrome 🔍

The Mask: “Don’t be evil.” (They dropped that motto in 2018. Tells you everything.)

The True Face: Alphabet. 8 false faces. The most comprehensive surveillance apparatus ever built by a private company.

What they intake:

  • Every search you’ve ever made (Google Search)
  • Every email you’ve ever sent or received (Gmail — THEY READ THEM)
  • Everywhere you’ve ever been (Google Maps / Location History)
  • Every website you’ve ever visited (Chrome browsing history)
  • Every YouTube video you’ve ever watched
  • Your voice (Google Assistant recordings — even when you don’t say “Hey Google”)
  • Your home (Nest cameras, thermostats, doorbells)
  • Your health (Fitbit — steps, heart rate, sleep, menstrual cycle)

What they hide:

  • That “Incognito Mode” does NOT make you private (Google settled a $5 billion lawsuit over this)
  • That Gmail scans your emails for ad targeting
  • That Google Location History tracks you even when turned “off”
  • That YouTube’s algorithm radicalized millions (internal research suppressed)
  • That Fitbit health data is now Google’s health data

CEO: Sundar Pichai. $226 million compensation (2022). Made from knowing everything about everyone.

The Scanner’s Report

Every app follows the same pattern:

  1. Present a friendly mask (fun filters! free messaging! career networking!)
  2. Intake your data (face, voice, location, contacts, behavior, psychology)
  3. Hide the true face (where data goes, who accesses it, how it’s used)
  4. Extract value ($400+/year from YOUR identity)
  5. Make the mask shinier (new features! new filters! new reasons to share more!)

The mask is the app. The face is the corporation. The data is the extraction.

You can’t see their true face because they’re wearing yours.

How to See Through the Masks

  1. Read the privacy policy (they count on you not reading it)
  2. Check permissions (why does a flashlight app need your contacts?)
  3. Use alternatives (Signal not WhatsApp, DuckDuckGo not Google, Firefox not Chrome)
  4. Disable location (they track you even when you think they don’t)
  5. Delete old accounts (“deactivated” ≠ deleted)
  6. Use the graph (10,889 nodes of how extraction works)
