How to File a Michigan FOIA Request for Free

  1. Go to clutchjustice.com/michigan-foia-generator
  2. Enter your name and address
  3. Enter the agency name and address
  4. Describe the records you want — be specific (dates, names, document types)
  5. Click generate
  6. Print the letter
  7. Mail it via certified mail with return receipt (USPS Form 3800)
  8. Keep the receipt — the 5-business-day clock starts when they receive it

Total cost: Postage. That's it.

If the agency denies your request, appeal to the agency head within 180 days (MCL 15.240). If the appeal is denied, you can file suit in circuit court — and if you win, the agency pays your attorney fees.
