## The Authorizer

Central Michigan University is located in Mount Pleasant, Michigan. Population: 21,000.

From Mount Pleasant, CMU authorized a charter school in Detroit — 150 miles away — founded by a man with 9 criminal convictions.

They didn't check.

---

## What CMU Does

CMU is one of Michigan's **charter school authorizers.** Under PA 362 (the 1993 charter school law [written with Dykema's involvement](/analysis/how-to-build-an-os/)), universities can authorize charter schools anywhere in the state.

CMU is the **largest charter school authorizer** in Michigan. They authorize more charter schools than any other institution.

They authorized **Purpose Charter Academy.**
They authorized **MacDowell Preparatory Academy.**

Both schools are run by Brian Banks.

---

## The Background Check

CMU did not perform a criminal background check on Brian Banks before authorizing his schools.

Brian Banks has 9 criminal convictions.

For context:

| Role | Background Check Required? |
|------|--------------------------|
| Substitute teacher | **Yes** |
| Bus driver | **Yes** |
| Cafeteria worker | **Yes** |
| Janitor | **Yes** |
| School volunteer | **Yes** (in most districts) |
| **Charter school founder** | **No** (CMU didn't require it) |

Every adult who enters a Michigan school building in a paid capacity needs a background check. Except the person who **creates** the school.

The person who decides what the children learn, who teaches them, how the money is spent, and whether the lights stay on — that person walks through without a check.

Because CMU didn't ask.

## The Revenue

CMU receives a portion of charter school per-pupil funding as the authorizer.

This means: CMU has a **financial incentive** to authorize charter schools. More schools = more revenue for CMU.

CMU has a **financial disincentive** to revoke authorization. Fewer schools = less revenue for CMU.

The entity that should be checking Banks benefits financially from not checking Banks.

## 150 Miles

CMU is 150 miles from Detroit.

From 150 miles away, they authorized a school and collected a check.
