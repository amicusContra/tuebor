RICO — The Legal Framework

Michigan RICO — MCL 750.159i

“A person employed by, or associated with, an enterprise shall not conduct or participate in the affairs of the enterprise directly or indirectly through a pattern of racketeering activity.”

Elements required:

  1. An enterprise — any individual, partnership, corporation, association, or group of individuals associated in fact (MCL 750.159f(a))
  2. Employment or association with the enterprise
  3. Conduct or participation in the enterprise’s affairs
  4. Through a pattern of racketeering activity — at least 2 incidents of racketeering activity, one of which occurred within 5 years of the other (MCL 750.159f(c))

Penalty: Up to 20 years imprisonment (MCL 750.159i)

Federal RICO — 18 U.S.C. § 1962

“It shall be unlawful for any person employed by or associated with any enterprise engaged in, or the activities of which affect, interstate or foreign commerce, to conduct or participate, directly or indirectly, in the conduct of such enterprise’s affairs through a pattern of racketeering activity.”

Elements: Same core structure. Federal RICO adds mail fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1341), wire fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343), and money laundering (18 U.S.C. § 1956) as predicates.

Penalty: Up to 20 years per count, plus forfeiture.
