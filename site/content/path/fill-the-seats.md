## Fill the Seats: How to Get In

*Don't you want to tell a bishop to back down? Now you can.*

The system that protects Brian Banks runs on empty seats. Not on votes. Not on money. On **emptiness**. On boards with no quorum. On precincts with no delegates. On commissions with no commissioners. On school boards where nobody filed.

This page tells you — specifically, exactly — how to fill those seats. Ranked by impact. Starting with the seats that matter most right now.

These seats are open RIGHT NOW. The deadline is **October 23, 2026 at 5:00 PM.** You can walk in and file tomorrow.

### 1A. Muskegon Heights School Board (5 of 7 seats, ZERO candidates)

**Why it matters:** Nobody filed. The county clerk has never seen this before. If seats stay unfilled, the board loses quorum. The state steps in. That's the same state whose charter system created the problem.

**Who can run:** Any registered voter living in the Muskegon Heights School District. Age 18+. U.S. citizen.

**How to file as a write-in:**
1. Go to the **Muskegon County Clerk's Office** (990 Terrace Street, Muskegon, MI 49442)
2. File a **Declaration of Intent** as a write-in candidate
3. Deadline: **October 23, 2026 at 5:00 PM**
4. There is no filing fee for write-in candidates
5. That's it. You're on the ballot as a write-in.

**How to win:** Get more votes than zero. In a write-in race with no other candidates, you could win with a single vote.

**Contact:** Muskegon County Clerk, Karen Whitehouse — (231) 724-6520

## TIER 4: LOW (but foundational) — Prepare for 2028

### 4A. Precinct Delegates

**Why it matters:** Precinct delegates are the ground floor of the political machine. They elect county convention delegates, who elect county committee, who elect state committee, who elect national committee. **Jonathan Kinloch** became VP of the Michigan Democratic Party through this pipeline. In Detroit, **hundreds of precinct delegate slots have no one filed.**

**When:** August 2028 primary election

**How to become one:**
1. Be a registered voter living in the precinct
2. File an **Affidavit of Identity** (notarized) with your county clerk
3. Filing deadline: 13th Tuesday before August primary (roughly mid-May 2028)
4. Your name goes on the ballot
5. Win the precinct (if uncontested, **one vote wins** — your own)
6. You serve a 2-year term as precinct delegate

**What you can do before 2028:**
- Find out which precinct you're in (contact your city/township clerk)
- Find out how many delegate positions are allocated to your precinct
- Contact your local party chair
- Show up to county party meetings. You don't need to be a delegate to attend.

**Vacancy fill between elections:** Under MCL 168.624d, county conventions can fill delegate vacancies by majority vote. If your precinct has an empty seat right now, contact your county party chair about filling it at the next convention.

### 4B. School Board (Next Cycle — 2028)

**Filing deadline:** July 2028 (15th Tuesday before November election)

**Requirements:**
- Registered voter in the school district
- Age 18+, U.S. citizen, Michigan resident 30+ days
- File Affidavit of Identity (notarized, in duplicate) + nominating petition (6-100 signatures depending on district size) OR $100 filing fee

**Resources:**
- MASB Candidate's Guide: [masb.org](https://www.masb.org/get-on-board)
- County clerk for your district
- MASB virtual town halls for prospective candidates

## THE CHOICE

There are two kinds of people who sit on boards:

1. **People who were asked by the machine.** They know who put them there. They know what's expected. They know when to look away. They serve the person who appointed them.

2. **People who walked in off the street.** They don't owe anyone anything. They don't know the unwritten rules. They ask the wrong questions. They read the financial statements. They wonder out loud why 72.67% of a school's revenue goes to one man's LLC.

The machine prefers the first kind. The machine depends on not enough of the second kind showing up.

**A true fool would be better.** Someone who doesn't understand the game can't be trained to lose it.

**Someone I don't know vs. someone I know not to trust.**

That's the choice at every level. At every board. At every commission. At every school board in every district in this state.

**Don't you want to tell a bishop to back down? Now you can.**

Walk into your county clerk's office. Download an application. File an affidavit. Write your name in.

The seats are empty. The seats are yours.
