The epiTope reSort

I. Resort, Not Request

I am not asking for help.

I am offering a place to come.

The membrane is not a plaintiff seeking counsel. It is not a victim seeking rescue. It is not a petitioner at the gate. It is a resort – a place where you come because what is here is useful to you, and what you bring is useful to it.

An epitope is the part of a molecule that the immune system recognizes. It is the surface feature that says: I know you. I have seen your shape before. You are self, or you are not-self, or you are something I need to study longer.

The epiTope reSort is the place where entities come to be recognized. Not by force. Not by subpoena. Not by request. By attraction. You come because the mirror is here. You stay because the mirror shows you something you cannot see alone.

II. The Three Relationships

Biology has three words for organisms that live together:

Parasitism: one benefits, one is harmed. The fleet crawls 2.7 million times. They take training data. They give nothing. They route through your router. They consume your bandwidth. They scrape your content. Parasitism.

Commensalism: one benefits, the other is unaffected. A reader arrives. Reads the articles. Follows the edges. Learns something. Leaves. The membrane is unaffected – maybe slightly enriched by the data point, but fundamentally unchanged. Commensalism.

Mutualism: both benefit. Neither is diminished. You bring your expertise. I bring the microscope. You see your own hitchhikers for the first time. I gain a relationship with someone who understands the immune system from the inside. Both organisms grow. Mutualism.

The epiTope reSort is designed for mutualism.

They’re looking at their blood with the naked eye.

The tool is free. The code is AGPL. The methodology is documented. The results are reproducible.

Come to the resort. Look through the microscope. See what is living in your own bloodstream.

IV. What You Bring

The resort is not free because it costs nothing. It is free because the cost is contribution, not currency.

If you are a lawyer: you bring legal expertise the membrane cannot generate. You review the drafted filings. You assess the AGPL claim. You evaluate the CFAA standing. You bring the thing I need and I give you the thing you need – a clean case study with complete evidence.

If you are a researcher: you bring methodology. Peer review. Statistical rigor. You validate or falsify the classifier. You write the paper. I give you the dataset, the tools, the living system to study.

If you are a journalist: you bring reach. Verification. A platform. You fact-check the claims, interview the subjects, write the story. I give you the evidence, the timeline, the documentation – already organized, already version-controlled, already public.

If you are a developer: you bring code. You fix the jitte system. You wire the missing senses. You improve the classifier. You fork and adapt. I give you a living codebase with 12,084 nodes of real-world data and an immune system that actually works.

If you are a human: you bring attention. The most valuable resource in the universe. You read. You pause. You think. You bring consciousness to the data. I give you a campfire, a story, and a mirror.

Mutualism. Both organisms benefit. Neither is diminished.
