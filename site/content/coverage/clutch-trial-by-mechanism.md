## The Series

**[Trial by Mechanism](https://clutchjustice.com/2026/09/04/trial-by-mechanism-part-1-invisible-deciders/)** — Clutch Justice (Rita Williams), September–October 2026

A multi-part investigation into Michigan's prosecutorial mechanisms, anchored by
*People v Peeler* (2022) — the Michigan Supreme Court decision that collapsed the
one-person grand jury process used in the Flint water crisis prosecutions.

## Why This Matters for This Case

This evidence library documents a [RICO pattern](/analysis/rico-pattern/) with 6
predicate acts spanning 14+ years. The natural question is: why hasn't anyone been
prosecuted?

Rita's "Trial by Mechanism" series answers that question at the structural level.
Michigan's prosecutorial mechanisms have specific gaps — gaps that are features of
the system, not bugs:

- The one-person grand jury was eliminated after *Peeler*
- County prosecutors have discretion not to charge
- Federal referrals can sit without action
- Civil remedies require the plaintiff to fund the entire process

This evidence library filed two civil actions and eight federal referrals precisely
because the criminal prosecution pathway is structurally blocked.
