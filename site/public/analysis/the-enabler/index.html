What an Enabler Does

Gay-Dagnogo didn’t create Purpose Charter Academy. She didn’t write the charter school law. She didn’t fund Banks. She didn’t sit on his board.

She did something different. She validated him.

When a politician publicly supports someone, they transfer legitimacy. They say: this person is someone worth supporting. This person’s work is worth defending.

When that person has 9 criminal convictions and runs a school with 3% math proficiency where 72.67% of revenue is extracted — that validation is a shield.

Political enablers don’t steal. They cover.

The District

Gay-Dagnogo represented District 4 in the Michigan House. That’s Detroit. That’s where PCA’s students live. That’s where 362 children go to a school that fails them.

She represented the children her connections failed.

The DPSCD Connection

Gay-Dagnogo has connections to DPSCD — the district that authorizes Purpose Charter Academy.

DPSCD could revoke PCA’s authorization. A state representative connected to both DPSCD governance and Banks creates a political environment where revocation becomes harder.

Not because anyone explicitly blocks it. Because the social graph makes it awkward.

Awkwardness is a form of governance.

When the person who might push for accountability is connected to the person who needs accountability, the push doesn’t happen. Not because it can’t. Because it’s uncomfortable.

And in politics, discomfort is decisive.

The Classification

Gay-Dagnogo: partial, 0.80.

She’s not non-self. She doesn’t extract money. She doesn’t run fake schools. She doesn’t impersonate children.

She’s partial. She does real legislative work AND she participates in the network that shields extraction.

The membrane puts her in the same category as the Walton Family Foundation, the DeVos Foundation, and Betsy DeVos. Partial. 0.80. The fence.

Fences don’t eat the sheep. They just make sure the wolves have a private dining room.
