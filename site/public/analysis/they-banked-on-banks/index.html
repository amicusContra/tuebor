What Parents Should Know

  1. You have rights. Michigan law guarantees custodial parents access to their children’s school records (FERPA, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g) and access to their children during school hours consistent with custody orders.

  2. “I am an attorney” is not a legal barrier. Brian Roderick Banks is not an attorney. His claim has no legal force. If PCA staff deny you access, ask for the specific legal basis in writing.

  3. PPOs require evidence. A Personal Protection Order requires a showing of cause. Filing a PPO in response to a parent exercising custody rights may constitute abuse of process.

  4. Document everything. Date, time, who said what, badge numbers if police are called. The DPD report from August 29 exists because someone documented it.

  5. File complaints. DPSCD authorized this school. Michigan Department of Education oversees charter authorizers. If DPSCD does not act, MDE can suspend their authorization power under MCL 380.502.
