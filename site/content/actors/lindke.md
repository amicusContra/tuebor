## The "Through My Eyes" Platform

"Through My Eyes" is a Facebook group with approximately **21,000 members** at its peak. Lindke used it to:

1. **Post victims' phone numbers** — leading to harassing calls from strangers
2. **Post PPO petitions** with personal contact information
3. **Coordinate follow-up harassment** — Judge Monaghan found posts were *"clearly designed to encourage his followers to harass the victims"*
4. **Make false accusations** — accused Miner of allowing their daughter contact with a convicted sex offender (court found accusations *"were false and [he] knew them to be false"*)
5. **Target followers against specific people** — City Manager James Freed was targeted; Freed testified Lindke *"harassed people, lots of PPOs"*

**Philip L. Ellison has been a member of Through My Eyes since July 6, 2020** — the same group Lindke used for the conduct that resulted in criminal conviction.

## The Federal Cases

### Lindke v. Freed (US Supreme Court)

Lindke sued Port Huron City Manager James Freed after Freed deleted Lindke's comments from his personal Facebook page and blocked him. The case went to the U.S. Supreme Court on the question of when a public official's social media page becomes a public forum.

**The Supreme Court ruled in Lindke's favor** — holding that a public official's social media page can be a public forum subject to First Amendment protections. This is a legitimately important First Amendment decision.

**The irony:** The same person who won a Supreme Court case about being blocked on social media was convicted of using social media to coordinate harassment. He fought for the right to comment on a public official's page while using his own page to post victims' phone numbers.

### Lindke v. King (Federal PPO challenge)

Ellison filed federal § 1983 challenges to the PPO statute on Lindke's behalf. Ellison **withdrew** from these cases on April 23, 2026 — the same day Lindke was booked in St. Clair County. The case was dismissed with prejudice on May 6, 2026.

## The Financial Architecture

Per [Clutch Justice, May 11, 2026](https://clutchjustice.com/2026/05/11/lindke-ellison-litigation-scheme/):

The federal PPO challenges pursued § 1983 claims that could generate **§ 1988 attorney's fees** for Ellison if successful. The SLAPP suit silences the journalist who documented the conduct underlying the PPOs → which undermines the PPOs → which supports the federal challenge → which generates fees.

**The structure collapsed** when Ellison withdrew and the federal case was dismissed with prejudice.
