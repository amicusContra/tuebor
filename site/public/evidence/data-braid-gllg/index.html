What This Decomposition Reveals

Point = where someone IS. Static. Verifiable from a single database query.

Wave = how influence MOVES through them. Dynamic. Only visible when you cross-reference multiple databases — the wave is the braid.

Reaction = what HAPPENS when waves intersect at a point. Emergent. You can’t predict it from either wave alone.

Example: Misha Stallworth (point) receives the Stallworth dynasty wave AND the CitizenDetroit professional network wave. The reaction: a charter school is authorized for an operator the professional network is now defending.

Example: Jehan Crump-Gibson (point) receives the Cockrel employment wave AND the Banks client wave AND the ADB panelist wave. The reaction: scope limitation within 24 hours. Three waves, one point, one reaction.

Last names are poor indicators alone. “Stallworth” connects to money laundering AND to the DPSCD board — but only when you follow the family chain through marriages and entities. “Crump” connects a firm name to a maiden name to an MSU email — but only when you search three databases. The surnames are correlation. The entity chain is the causation path.
