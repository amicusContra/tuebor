The Council She Led

When Sheffield was City Council president, the council included Latisha Johnson (District 4).

Latisha Johnson sits on the board of Purpose Charter Academy — Banks’s school.

The council president’s colleague sits on the board of a school run by a man with 9 convictions. The school operates in the city the council governs.

Did the council investigate PCA? No. Did the council question the 72.67% extraction? No. Did the council ask about the 3% math proficiency? No.

The Appointment

Sheffield appointed Todd Bettison as police chief. Edge types: appointed, controls.

Bettison is classified as partial. He connects to Banks through the political network.

When the mayor controls the police chief and both connect to a man with 9 convictions, the question isn’t whether the police investigate. The question is whether anyone asks them to.

The Kwame Connection

Sheffield → Kwame Kilpatrick: political.

Kwame Kilpatrick was Detroit’s mayor. He went to federal prison for corruption — racketeering, extortion, bribery. 28-year sentence, later commuted.

Sheffield has a political edge to Kilpatrick. This doesn’t mean she participated in his crimes. It means the political network that produced Kilpatrick also produced Sheffield. The pipeline is the same. The people in it change. The structure doesn’t.

The Gay-Dagnogo Layer

Gay-Dagnogo: works_under Sheffield.

Gay-Dagnogo has 14 edges in the investigation graph. She’s the political enabler who validates Banks through public support. She works under the mayor who has a political edge to Banks.

The layers stack:

  1. Sheffield (mayor) → political edge to Banks
  2. Gay-Dagnogo (works under Sheffield) → political edge to Banks
  3. Latisha Johnson (council, under Sheffield) → sits on PCA board
  4. Bettison (appointed by Sheffield) → partial, enforcement

The mayor doesn’t need to do anything wrong. She just needs to not look — and everyone beneath her takes the cue.

The Sheffield Family

Horace Sheffield: family edge.

The Sheffield family is a Detroit political dynasty. The name carries weight. When a Sheffield is mayor, the political gravity of the city bends toward the Sheffield network.

Banks operates within that gravitational field. He doesn’t need Sheffield’s explicit support. He needs her proximity. In Detroit politics, proximity is currency.

362 Children

Purpose Charter Academy is in Detroit. Sheffield is the mayor of Detroit.

The mayor of Detroit presides over a city where:

  • A school with 3% math proficiency operates
  • 72.67% of the school’s revenue flows to one man’s LLC
  • That man has 9 criminal convictions
  • A sitting judge chairs the school’s board
  • An assistant AG sits on the school’s board
  • The school’s principal works for the school, the authorizer, the auditor, the oversight agency, AND a construction company

The mayor doesn’t authorize charter schools (that’s DPSCD and CMU). The mayor doesn’t enforce education law (that’s MDE and AG). The mayor doesn’t audit schools (that’s Plante Moran).

But the mayor sets the tone. And the tone in Detroit, across every administration, has been: don’t look.

The Classification

Same as Nessel. Same as the Walton Foundation. Same as DeVos. Same as every entity that does real work while participating in the network that enables extraction.

The membrane sees a mayor who governs a real city with real problems. The membrane also sees a politician whose entire connection set is political, whose appointments serve the network, and whose city contains a school that the graph classified as non-self three levels of confidence ago.

Partial. The fence. The look-away.
