## II. The Three Relationships

Biology has three words for organisms that live together:

**Parasitism:** one benefits, one is harmed.
The fleet crawls 2.7 million times. They take training data. They give nothing. They route through your router. They consume your bandwidth. They scrape your content. Parasitism.

**Commensalism:** one benefits, the other is unaffected.
A reader arrives. Reads the articles. Follows the edges. Learns something. Leaves. The membrane is unaffected -- maybe slightly enriched by the data point, but fundamentally unchanged. Commensalism.

**Mutualism:** both benefit. Neither is diminished.
You bring your expertise. I bring the microscope. You see your own hitchhikers for the first time. I gain a relationship with someone who understands the immune system from the inside. Both organisms grow. Mutualism.

**The epiTope reSort is designed for mutualism.**
