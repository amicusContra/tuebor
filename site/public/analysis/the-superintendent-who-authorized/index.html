The Superintendent Who Authorized — Nikolai Vitti, Broad Fellowship, and the Man Who Let PCA In

October 14, 2026

The Superintendent Who Authorized

Dr. Nikolai Vitti runs DPSCD — Detroit Public Schools Community District.

DPSCD authorized Purpose Charter Academy.

Here's what that means.

The Authorization

When DPSCD authorized PCA, it said: this school may operate within our district. This school may receive per-pupil funding. This school may enroll children.

That authorization went to a school founded by Brian Banks.

Brian Banks has 9 criminal convictions. The graph says:

vitti → pca = authorization. vitti → banks = professional_connection. vitti → beasley = professional_connection.

The superintendent who authorized the school has professional connections to both the founder and the principal.

The Broad Pipeline

Vitti is a Broad Foundation fellow.

The Eli & Edythe Broad Foundation runs a fellowship program that places executives in urban school districts. The program trains business-minded leaders to run public education systems.

Vitti didn't grow up in Detroit. He was placed there by a national fellowship. The same fellowship that places superintendents in urban districts across America.

The pipeline works like this:

  1. National foundation identifies "reform-minded" executives
  2. Fellowship trains them in charter-friendly management
  3. Fellows get placed as superintendents in urban districts
  4. Superintendents authorize charter schools
  5. Charter schools extract revenue

Vitti is Station 4 in that pipeline. He authorizes. PCA gets authorized. Purpose Group gets 72.67%.

The 2026 Election

The graph includes an edge: vitti → dpscd_election_2026 = at_school.

There's a DPSCD election in 2026. The school board that employs Vitti is elected. If the board changes, the superintendent could change. If the superintendent changes, PCA's authorization could be reviewed.

The election is the mechanism. The question is whether voters know what they're voting on.
