The Standard

Michigan's Code of Judicial Conduct (Canon 2) requires judges to avoid the appearance of impropriety.

Canon 3(C)(1) requires disqualification when impartiality might reasonably be questioned.

Canon 4 restricts extrajudicial activities that would cast doubt on capacity to act impartially.

Chairing the board of a school run by a convicted person: appearance of impropriety.

Paying campaign funds to a convicted person's LLCs: reasonable question of impartiality.

Being family to the principal of a school under investigation: doubt on capacity to act impartially.

The standards exist. The code exists. The canons exist.

The judges exist on both sides of every canon they're supposed to uphold.
