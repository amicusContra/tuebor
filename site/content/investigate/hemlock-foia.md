## What This Page Is

Ready-to-use FOIA request templates targeting specific Saginaw County agencies. Each template identifies the records, cites the statutory authority, and explains what the responsive documents would reveal.

**You do not need a lawyer to file a FOIA request.** Michigan's Freedom of Information Act (MCL 15.231 et seq.) gives every person the right to inspect and copy public records. You do not need to state a reason. The agency must respond within 5 business days.

**Do not use FOIAworks** for any of these requests. See [why](/evidence/foiaworks-honeypot/).

---

## How Michigan FOIA Works

| Step | What Happens | Statute |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **1. Submit request** | Written request identifying the records. Email is acceptable. | MCL 15.232(2) |
| **2. Agency responds** | Within 5 business days. Must grant, deny, or request 10-day extension. | MCL 15.235(2) |
| **3. If denied** | Written denial with specific exemption cited. You can appeal to agency head. | MCL 15.235(4) |
| **4. If appeal denied** | You can file suit in circuit court. Prevailing requesters get attorney's fees. | MCL 15.240 |
| **5. Fee disputes** | Agency can charge labor + copying. You can challenge unreasonable fees. | MCL 15.234 |

## Template 1: Saginaw ISD — Contracts with Ellison Entities

**Target:** Saginaw Intermediate School District
**FOIA Coordinator:** Check [sisd.cc](https://www.sisd.cc) for current coordinator
**Why this matters:** Katie Ellison is **President** of the Saginaw ISD Board of Education (term 2021–2027; [sisd.cc](https://www.sisd.cc/article/2617791)). Her husband Philip Ellison operates OLC PLC, Quagmire Solutions LLC, and South Brennan Property LLC. Any contracts between the ISD and Ellison-controlled entities would represent the **board president's spouse** profiting from the district.

```

FROM: [Your Name]
DATE: [Date]

RE: Freedom of Information Act Request — MCL 15.231 et seq.

I request copies of the following public records:

1. All contracts, agreements, invoices, purchase orders, and payment
   records between Saginaw ISD and any of the following entities or
   persons, from January 1, 2015 to present:

   a. Outside Legal Counsel PLC
   b. Philip L. Ellison
   c. Quagmire Solutions LLC
   d. South Brennan Property LLC
   e. FOIAworks or foiaworks.com

2. All board meeting minutes where Katherine (Katie) Ellison recused
   herself from any vote, discussion, or action, from the date of her
   appointment to present.

3. All board meeting minutes where any contract with, payment to, or
   relationship with Philip L. Ellison, Outside Legal Counsel PLC,
   Quagmire Solutions LLC, or South Brennan Property LLC was discussed,
   voted on, or referenced.

4. The ISD's current conflict of interest policy and any signed conflict
   of interest disclosures filed by Katherine Ellison.

I request electronic copies where available. I am willing to pay
reasonable copying fees up to $25.00 without prior approval. Please
contact me before proceeding if fees will exceed that amount.

Per MCL 15.235(2), please respond within five business days.

[Your Name]
[Your Email]
[Your Address]
```

**What to look for in the response:**
- Any contracts with Ellison entities = board member's spouse profiting from district
- Absence of recusal records = board member voting on matters affecting spouse's interests
- Absence of conflict disclosures = policy compliance failure

## Tips for Filing

1. **Keep it specific.** Name the exact records, entities, and date ranges. Vague requests ("all records about Philip Ellison") give agencies an excuse to delay.

2. **Use email.** Michigan courts have held that email is a valid method for submitting FOIA requests. Keep the email as your receipt.

3. **Screenshot the submission.** Date-stamped proof that you filed starts the 5-day clock.

4. **If they ask for money up front,** you can ask them to narrow the scope or to provide a detailed fee estimate. MCL 15.234 requires the estimate to be itemized.

5. **If they deny,** the denial must cite a specific FOIA exemption. "We don't have that" is an acceptable response — if true. "We won't look" is not.

6. **Appeal denials** to the head of the public body (superintendent, board chair, county administrator). If the appeal is denied, you can file suit under MCL 15.240.

7. **Document everything.** Save every email, every response, every denial. The paper trail IS the evidence.

8. **Share what you find.** If you receive responsive documents that reveal conflicts, vendor relationships, or policy failures, you can submit them to this investigation through [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/amicusContra/tuebor/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) or via hello@clutchjustice.com.
