## The Question Clutch Raised

> Michigan law requires a charter school ranked in the bottom 5 percent for three
> consecutive years to close (MCL 380.507). Has this provision been triggered?

This evidence library's [analysis section](/analysis/) traces why it hasn't:
the authorizer (DPSCD) has financial incentive to keep the charter open (3%
authorization fee ≈ $81,000/year), and the board members have network connections
to the operator. The oversight loop is captured.
