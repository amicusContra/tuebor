Domain Registration Campaign: Three PPO Holders Targeted in 30 Minutes

Summary

On September 9, 2025, three domain names containing the full legal names of people with active Personal Protection Orders against Kevin Lindke were registered within a 30-minute window. All three used the same registrar (GoDaddy), the same privacy service (Domains By Proxy), and the same lock configuration.

Less than 24 hours later, the domain for Philip L. Ellison's law firm (olcplc.com) received a registry update — consistent with a single GoDaddy account performing bulk operations across all domains in the account.

One domain targets a minor child.

As of October 6, 2026, the domain ritafelinewilliams.com has been renewed through September 2027 and hosts an active impersonation website.
