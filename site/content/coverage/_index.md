Independent reporters and investigative projects have covered the same people,
institutions, and systemic failures documented in this evidence library. This
section cross-references their work with ours.

**We are not the source of these stories. The reporters are.**
We are the index — the place where the threads converge.

---

## How This Works

Each entry links to an independent article and maps it to pages in this
evidence library that cover the same ground. If you came here from one of
these articles, the cross-references show you what else the public record
reveals. If you came here first, these articles provide independent
verification and context.

## Independent Projects

Some coverage comes from independent investigative projects — self-sovereign
work by reporters who fund their own infrastructure, control their own publishing,
and answer to no editor. We treat these projects as peers, not just sources.

- **[Clutch Justice — Rita Williams](/coverage/clutch-justice/)** — Michigan courts, crime, and judicial accountability. Our primary analytical reference. Four direct-overlap articles, plus statewide judicial accountability reporting that contextualizes everything in this database.

## Signal Categories

We cross-index external coverage in three categories:

1. **Direct overlap** — articles about the same people and entities we document
2. **Structural context** — articles about the Michigan systems (courts, authorizers, oversight) that enable the patterns we document
3. **Methodological** — articles about how public records work, how to obtain them, and what institutional record-keeping reveals
