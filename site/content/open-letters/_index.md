Each letter follows a strict transparency protocol:

1. **Draft** — committed to the public record with a cryptographic timestamp
2. **Send privately** — delivered to the recipient via documented channels
3. **Wait 72 hours** — recipient has time to respond privately
4. **Publish** — letter goes live here with delivery method and response status
5. **Update** — any response is published in full alongside the letter

Nobody can claim they weren't told. Nobody can claim the letters were unfair.
The full text is public.
