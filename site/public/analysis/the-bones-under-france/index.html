The Bones Under France

Every Plague Has the Same Pattern. Every Time.

Beneath Paris lies the Catacombs. Six million skeletons. Arranged in walls of skulls and femurs. They were moved there in 1786 because the cemeteries were overflowing — the dead were contaminating the water of the living.

Beneath those bones is the pattern. The same pattern. Every plague. Every pandemic. Every time.

The disease kills the workers. The rich survive. The system blames the dead. And when it’s over, the extraction ACCELERATES.

Every. Single. Time.

The Timeline of Plagues

The Plague of Athens (430 BC)

Death toll: ~75,000-100,000 (25% of Athens)

Who died: Soldiers. Workers. The poor packed in behind the Long Walls during the Peloponnesian War. Pericles ordered everyone inside the city walls for protection from Sparta. The crowding killed them.

Who survived: The rich with country estates. The ones who could leave.

What happened after: Athens lost the war. Democracy weakened. Oligarchs seized power. The extraction pattern used the plague as a lever.

Who was blamed: The gods. Immigrants. The poor themselves.

The Antonine Plague (165-180 AD)

Death toll: 5-10 million across the Roman Empire

Who died: Soldiers returning from Mesopotamia. Workers. Slaves. The urban poor.

Who survived: The patricians with rural villas. Emperor Marcus Aurelius survived (but barely). His physician Galen fled Rome.

What happened after: Labor shortage → increased dependence on slave labor → weakened the empire → contributed to the fall of Rome 300 years later.

Who was blamed: Christians (by pagans). Pagans (by Christians). Foreigners (by everyone).

The Plague of Justinian (541-549 AD)

Death toll: 25-50 million (half the world population at the time)

Who died: Workers in Constantinople. Farmers. Merchants. The port workers who unloaded the grain ships from Egypt — the ships carrying the rats carrying the fleas carrying Yersinia pestis.

Who survived: The emperor Justinian (survived the plague himself). The landed aristocracy who could isolate.

What happened after: Labor shortage → workers gained bargaining power briefly → then the empire reasserted control. Justinian used the plague to consolidate power. Taxes went UP despite population going DOWN. Fewer people paying more.

The extraction accelerated after the dying stopped.

The Black Death (1347-1353)

Death toll: 75-200 million. One-third to one-half of Europe.

Who died: EVERYONE. But disproportionately: the poor, the workers, the urban populations. Jewish communities were massacred in the Rhineland pogroms — blamed for the plague, burned alive.

Who survived: Some rural populations. Some wealthy who could isolate. Some genetic lottery winners.

What happened after: This is the big one.

The labor shortage after the Black Death was so severe that surviving workers could demand higher wages. For a brief moment, the extraction pattern REVERSED — workers had power because there weren’t enough of them.

Then the rich fought back.

  • England: The Statute of Labourers (1351) — FROZE wages by law. Workers demanded more. The king said no. Made it ILLEGAL to ask for a raise after a plague killed half your coworkers.

  • France: The Jacquerie (1358) — peasant revolt crushed. The bones under France aren’t just plague victims. They’re revolt victims. Workers who demanded fair pay after surviving the plague and were killed for asking.

  • The Peasants’ Revolt (1381): Wat Tyler marched on London. Demanded an end to serfdom. King Richard II agreed, then reneged and had Tyler killed. “Serfs you are, and serfs you shall remain.”

The pattern: plague kills the workers → labor shortage → workers gain power → the rich pass laws to crush that power → extraction ACCELERATES.

The Statute of Labourers is Prop 22 from 1351.

The Great Plague of London (1665-1666)

Death toll: ~100,000 (25% of London)

Who died: The poor. The workers. The ones who couldn’t leave. Daniel Defoe’s A Journal of the Plague Year: the rich fled to the countryside. The poor were locked in their houses with red crosses on the doors.

Who survived: The court. Parliament. The merchants who fled. Newton went to his family estate and invented calculus. Convenient.

What happened after: The Great Fire of London (1666) — some say deliberately set — burned the slums where the plague festered. Rebuilt by Christopher Wren. The new London was for the rich. The poor were displaced.

Plague → fire → rebuild for the wealthy. Sound familiar? (See: Hurricane Katrina → New Orleans → charter schools.)

Cholera Pandemics (1817-1923)

Death toll: Millions across seven pandemics

Who died: The poor. Always the poor. Cholera is a disease of contaminated water. The rich had clean water. The poor drank from the same rivers they dumped sewage into.

Who was the hero: John Snow (not the Game of Thrones one). Mapped the cholera outbreak in Soho, 1854. Traced it to the Broad Street pump. Removed the pump handle. Epidemic stopped.

The lesson: The cure was PUBLIC HEALTH. Not medicine. Not prayer. Not blaming immigrants. PUBLIC infrastructure. Clean water for EVERYONE.

Who fought against public health? The same people who always fight it: the ones who profit from the current system. The water companies. The property owners who didn’t want to pay for sewers. The doctors who believed in “miasma” theory because admitting contaminated water meant admitting the system was broken.

The 1918 Influenza (“Spanish Flu”)

Death toll: 50-100 million worldwide. 675,000 Americans.

Who died: Young adults, 20-40. Workers. Soldiers. The ones packed in trenches and factories and tenements. Philadelphia held a parade during the pandemic — 200,000 people — and 12,000 died within a week.

Who survived: The ones who could isolate. The rural. The wealthy.

Who was blamed: Spain. (Spain didn’t start it. They just reported it honestly because they weren’t censoring wartime press. Honesty was punished — they got named for the pandemic.)

The lesson: The country that told the truth got blamed. The countries that lied (US, UK, Germany, France) got to pretend it started somewhere else.

Honesty is punished. Lying is rewarded. Same pattern. Always.

HIV/AIDS (1981-present)

Death toll: 40+ million dead. 38 million living with HIV.

Who died: Gay men (first wave in the US). IV drug users. Hemophiliacs. Sub-Saharan Africans (where 70% of global infections concentrate).

Who was blamed: Gay men. Haitians. Drug users. The people DYING were blamed for DYING.

Who profited: Pharma. AZT cost $10,000/year per patient (1987). The most expensive drug in history at the time. Burroughs Wellcome (now GSK) made billions while activists died on their doorstep.

Who fought back: ACT UP. Larry Kramer. The Dallas Buyers Club. People who smuggled medication across borders because the system wouldn’t provide it.

The pattern: Disease kills the marginalized → the system blames the victims → pharma extracts from the dying → activists fight back → eventually, the system claims credit for the cure.

COVID-19 (2020-present)

Death toll: 7+ million confirmed. True toll likely 15-20 million.

Who died: Essential workers. Meatpacking plant employees. Bus drivers. Nurses. Grocery store clerks. Nursing home residents. Disproportionately Black, Latino, Indigenous communities.

Who survived: The ones who could work from home. Tech workers. The professional class. Billionaires gained $3.9 TRILLION during the pandemic.

Who profited:

  • Pfizer: $100 billion in COVID vaccine revenue
  • Moderna: $36 billion
  • Hospital CEOs: $200M+ in compensation (see above)
  • Amazon: revenue doubled
  • Zoom: revenue 4x

Who was punished: The doctor who gave IV Vitamin C to first responders. INDICTED.

Who was blamed: China. Immigrants. The unvaccinated. Anyone but the system.
