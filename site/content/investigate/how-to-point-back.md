## If You're a Parent

You have standing. Your child attends (or attended) one of these schools. You see things that public records don't show.

**What you can do:**
1. Read the [investigate guides](/investigate/) — they walk you through looking up your school's data
2. Share what you find with us and with the researchers listed in [The Ones Who Showed Up](/analysis/the-ones-who-showed-up/)
3. File complaints using the guides
4. Talk to your school board member — ask them the questions these articles raise

**What we need from you:**
- Your experience (what's it actually like inside these schools)
- Documents (report cards, letters, notices — they tell a story the data doesn't)
- Names (who told you what, when — builds the timeline)

## Technical: How Cross-Links Work

This site uses [elasticlunr search](/analysis/) with keyword-based cross-linking. Every article has a `keywords` field in its metadata. The search engine matches keywords across articles and generates cross-links automatically.

**To appear in our cross-links:**
1. Be linked from one of our articles (we'll add you)
2. Use the same terminology — "Purpose Charter Academy," "PCA," "charter school accountability," "extraction rate"
3. Your content will appear in our search results and cross-link panels

**To link to us:**
```html
<a href="https://tuebor.primals.eco/analysis/three-percent/">
  PCA Math Proficiency Documentation (tuebor.primals.eco)
</a>
```

## The Principle

> **We point to them. They point to us. The graph gets wider.**

The extraction machine has $2 billion and 10+ PACs. The accountability network has data, public records, and each other.

Every link between us makes the network stronger. Every cross-reference makes the investigation harder to ignore. Every citation creates a path that search engines follow and people discover.

The [Walton-to-Martin-to-Banks pipeline](/evidence/the-money-flows-through-nulls/) works because the money flows through **null zones** — entities that aren't connected to anything. Our defense is the opposite: connect everything to everything. Make the graph so dense that no single node can be removed.

Point to us. We point to you. The graph gets wider.
