The Ones Who Showed Up

This investigation didn't start from nothing. It started because people showed up. Documented. Filed. Published. Testified. Organized. Persisted.

Credit where credit is due.

Dr. Chalena Yancey Beasley

21 edges in the graph. More connections than most institutions.

Beasley mapped the terrain before anyone else drew the map. She co-investigated Anderson localization, Holland's network, the MDE oversight gaps, the Wayne RESA audit, and the Court 3rd connections. She traced the spiral down — proficiency, extraction, credential fraud — and then traced it back out to the regulatory bodies that could have acted and didn't.

Beasley asked the questions directly — to Banks, to Holland, to Roland Martin, to the AG, to the school board. She documented the negative space — no audit, no charges, no media, no whistleblower protection, no FOIA response, no resignation, no restitution.

She built the first stratum of the investigation. Everything that came after stands on what she mapped.

→ Dr. Beasley's work is referenced in 8 articles across the site.
