  • Address: 15955 State St, Hillman, MI 49746
  • Status: Operational
  • FCC Application: SESLIC2021030300425

Gateway earth stations maintain continuous Ka-band and Ku-band links with overhead satellites. When a user terminal communicates with a Starlink satellite, the signal is relayed down to the nearest gateway, which connects to the terrestrial internet backbone — the fiber.

The 112,096 ghost subnets trace back to VPS and proxy infrastructure — cloud providers, hosting companies, VPN services. Each subnet maps to an ASN (Autonomous System Number), which maps to a company, which maps to physical infrastructure — data centers, fiber, tower backhaul.

Every IP address is a physical connection. Every physical connection runs through infrastructure that is registered, licensed, and traceable.

What This Means

Every spam call hitting your phone traveled through this infrastructure. Every robocall originated from a registered VoIP provider, passed through licensed carriers, bounced off registered towers or satellites, and arrived through traceable fiber or radio links.

The FCC has the registration for every tower. The FCC has the license for every carrier. The FCC has the orbital filing for every satellite. The FCC has the earth station application for every ground station.

They know who sends the calls. Because they registered every piece of infrastructure that carries them.
