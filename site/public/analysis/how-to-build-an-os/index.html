The RFFW Complaint — A $1 Million Case Study

On July 14, 2022, RFFW LLC was formed in Michigan. Within 15 days, it donated $1,000,000 to Reproductive Freedom for All (the abortion rights ballot campaign). RFFW had no website, no public presence, and no disclosed donors.

After a campaign finance complaint, the Secretary of State's office concluded "there may be reason to believe" RFFW violated state campaign finance law. RFFW was forced to register, disclose that Shery Cotton (co-founder of Meridian Health Plan, sold for $2.5 billion) provided the $1M, and pay $1,300 in late fees.

The complaint characterized the arrangement as a "$1,000,000 money laundering scheme" — forming an LLC for the sole purpose of hiding a donor's identity from the public for 353 days.

RFFW's treasurer: Renae Moore (Dykema). RFFW's attorney: W. Alan Wilk (Dykema). RFFW's registered agent: CSC – Lawyers Incorporating Service. RFFW's compliance filings: Signed by Moore, addressed from Dykema's Lansing office.

Source: MI SOS complaint · Detroit News
