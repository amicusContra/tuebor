Sitting in a Tree

You know the rhyme.

Mike and Tom, sitting in a tree K-I-S-S-I-N-G

But who's the tree?

The tree is Dykema Gossett PLLC — Michigan's largest law firm. $200–300 million in annual revenue. Offices in six states and Washington, D.C. It doesn't pick sides. It grows branches for whoever pays.

Mike Rogers sits on one branch. Tom Barrett sits on another. Brian Banks — nine criminal convictions, superintendent of two charter schools, no bar admission for his claimed J.D. — sits on a third.

Same tree. Same roots. Same trunk. Same person watering all of it.
