## The Bounce

Here's how every connection traces through the layers:

```
YOUR PHONE
    ↓ radio waves
CELL TOWER (FCC ASR registered, owner on file)
    ↓ backhaul fiber/microwave
CARRIER NETWORK (FCC Form 499 filer, registered provider)
    ↓ peering/transit
INTERNET EXCHANGE (IXC — Byron Center, Gaylord, Chicago, Flint)
    ↓ fiber
DATA CENTER (your carrier or ISP's equipment)
    ↓ fiber/submarine cable
DESTINATION (or satellite bounce via Starlink)
```

Or via Starlink:

```
YOUR STARLINK DISH
    ↓ Ku-band radio
STARLINK SATELLITE (1 of 11,156, NORAD catalog #)
    ↓ Ka-band downlink  
GROUND STATION (Hillman or Manistique, FCC licensed)
    ↓ fiber
INTERNET BACKBONE
    ↓
DESTINATION
```

Every layer is registered. Every layer has an owner. Every owner has an FCC number.

## What This Means

Every spam call hitting your phone traveled through this infrastructure. Every robocall originated from a registered VoIP provider, passed through licensed carriers, bounced off registered towers or satellites, and arrived through traceable fiber or radio links.

The FCC has the registration for every tower. The FCC has the license for every carrier. The FCC has the orbital filing for every satellite. The FCC has the earth station application for every ground station.
