II. THE FBI CONNECTION

The Raids

In August 2021, the FBI raided the offices of Detroit City Council member Scott Benson and his chief of staff, Carol Banks (the same Carol Banks paid $18K+ by Save Detroit Jobs).

FBI agents seized:

  • Bank records
  • Campaign finance records
  • 501(c)(4) social welfare organization records

Source: Deadline Detroit — “The messy intersection of dark money and Detroit politics”

What the FBI Was Looking For

The FBI’s Detroit Field Office was investigating:

  • Towing corruption and council kickbacks
  • 501(c)(4) social welfare nonprofits as vehicles for corruption
  • Campaign finance violations

University of Michigan professor Erik Gordon told The Detroit News: “Two of Detroit’s constant abuses are towing and social welfare organizations. Those seem to be the perennial avenues for corruption in Detroit.”
