## Selective Enforcement

Michigan Attorney General Dana Nessel's office opened an investigation into Brian Banks.

So here's the question: **Why does her assistant AG sit on Brian Banks's school board?**

---

## The Name

**Dawn N. Ison, Esq.**

Dawn Ison is an assistant attorney general in the state of Michigan.

Dawn Ison also sits on the board of **Purpose Charter Academy** — the school founded by Brian Banks, a man with 9 criminal convictions, whose management company extracts 72.67% of the school's revenue.

The AG Office's edge to Banks: **investigation.**
Dawn Ison's edge to PCA: **board_membership, complicity_by_governance.**

The person investigating and the person enabling sit in the same building.

---

## The Downhill Problem

The AG's office enforces **downhill.**

They investigate:
- Brian Banks (non-self, 134 edges)
- Small charter operators
- Individual complaints

They do not investigate:
- **Dykema Gossett** (wrote the charter school law)
- **Walton Family Foundation** (funds the schools)
- **Plante Moran** (audits the schools, non-response)
- **NAACP Detroit** (advocates for Banks)
- **State Bar of Michigan** (ignores UPL complaints)
- **CMU** (authorizes the schools, no background checks)

This is the enforcement ecosystem: the people at the bottom of the pipeline get caught. The people who built the pipeline get thanked.

---

## The Charitable Trust Division

The AG Office has a **Charitable Trust Division** — AG Charitable Trust.

This division is supposed to oversee nonprofits. Purpose Foundation (the 501(c)(3) that owns Purpose Charter Academy) is a nonprofit.

The Charitable Trust Division has the power to:
- Audit nonprofits
- Investigate self-dealing
- Petition for dissolution
- Refer for criminal prosecution

The graph edge: `ag_charitable_trust → banks` = **investigation.**

But that investigation runs through the same office where Dawn Ison works. And Dawn Ison sits on the board of the school they're investigating.

**Recusal is not a substitute for structure.**

---

## The JTC Connection

The Judicial Tenure Commission (JTC) referred the Banks matter for potential investigation.

The JTC's investigator: **Michael Ison.**

Michael Ison is married to Dawn Ison.

The JTC investigator's spouse sits on the board of the school run by the man the JTC referred.

This is not a conspiracy. This is **Detroit.** The graph is small enough that everyone knows everyone, and big enough that no one thinks they need to recuse.
