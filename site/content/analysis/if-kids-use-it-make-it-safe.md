## The Standard

One question. Applied to everything a child touches.

**Is it safe?**

Not "is it profitable." Not "does it have parental controls buried six menus deep." Not "did they settle the FTC complaint quietly."

*Is it safe for a child?*

---

## 🟢 SAFE — Built for Children, Not for Extraction

### PBS Kids / Sesame Workshop
Free. No ads. No data collection. No algorithmic manipulation. Educational content designed by child development researchers. **The gold standard since 1969.** Sesame Street taught more children to read than any school system. They didn't need a management LLC taking 72.67%.

### Khan Academy / Khan Academy Kids
Free. No ads. No paywalls. Sal Khan serves 150 million students because he believes education is a right. Khan Academy Kids is designed for ages 2-8 with zero extraction. **Proof that world-class education costs $0.**

### Scratch (MIT Media Lab)
Free coding platform for children. Open source. No monetization of users. MIT built it because children deserve to create, not just consume. **23M+ projects created by kids.**

### Reading Rainbow / LeVar Burton Kids
LeVar Burton's literacy mission. "You don't have to take my word for it." The app continues his work of making reading irresistible. **Verify-first education.**

### Miss Rachel / Songs for Littles
Speech therapy meets children's television. Free on YouTube. Rachel Accurso teaches children to speak their first words. Parents trust her with the most important thing they have. **10M+ families served.**

### Blue's Clues (Legacy)
Steve Burns talked directly to children. He waited for their answers. He treated every child watching as a real person. **The model: one human, one child, total presence.**

### Common Sense Media
Rates every piece of media for age-appropriateness. Free reviews for parents. Advocacy for children's digital safety legislation. **The compass.**

### Imagination Library (Dolly Parton)
200M+ free physical books mailed to children. No screen. No algorithm. No data collection. A book shows up. **The purest model.**

## 🔴 UNSAFE — Extraction Disguised as Play

### Roblox
$4.89B revenue (2025). 75% platform take. Children working 40+ hours/week creating content for $0 real dollars. Digital company scrip. CEO Dave Baszucki worth $3.4B — **built on unpaid child labor.** Vanderbilt Law Review: "Minors or Miners?" Federal class action filed May 2026. *([Full analysis →](/analysis/eat-roblox/))*

### YouTube (for children)
**$170M FTC fine** (2019) for COPPA violations — illegally collecting data on children under 13. Elsagate (2017): algorithmic pipelines serving disturbing content disguised as children's videos. YouTube Kids launched as a "fix" but still runs on engagement algorithms, not child safety. Autoplay = infinite scroll for toddlers. The same platform that hosts Miss Rachel also hosts the algorithm that exploits her audience. **Google's annual revenue: $307B. The fine was 0.05% of one year's revenue.**

### TikTok
Addictive by design. Average teen spends 95 minutes/day. Algorithm learns what makes a child's brain light up, then feeds more. Internal documents show ByteDance knew about harm to minors. Multiple state AG lawsuits filed 2023-2026. **Designed to be impossible to put down. Already in the blackwall.**

### Instagram (Meta)
Internal research (leaked by Frances Haugen, 2021): "We make body image issues worse for one in three teen girls." Meta knew. Meta continued. Instagram for Kids was shelved after public outrage but the main platform still targets teenagers with engagement-optimized content. **Already in the blackwall via Meta.**

### Snapchat
Disappearing messages = feature for predators. My AI chatbot gave inappropriate responses to minors (2023 reports). Snap Map shows real-time location of children to anyone who adds them. NCMEC reports Snapchat as a top platform for child exploitation. **Designed around ephemerality — which means designed around unaccountability.**

### Discord
Voice + text + screen sharing with strangers. Used by gaming communities, including children as young as 8. Server moderation is community-dependent — no central safety. Grooming, explicit content, radicalization pipelines documented by researchers. **An open door with no one watching.**

### Fortnite (Epic Games)
Loot boxes removed after regulatory pressure, but V-Bucks economy still targets children's psychology. $520M FTC settlement (2022) — largest gaming enforcement in FTC history. Voice chat with strangers enabled by default for years. **Epic paid more in FTC fines than Banks' schools receive in a decade.**

### Kick
Gambling streams watched by minors. Minimal age verification. Launched as the "anti-Twitch" but with even fewer child protections. **Gambling marketed to children under a content creator wrapper.**

### Telegram
Minimal moderation. CSAM distribution documented by NCMEC and IWF. End-to-end encryption with no reporting mechanism for child exploitation content. **Privacy for adults. Danger for children.**

## 🟡 MIXED — Could Go Either Way

### Minecraft (Microsoft/Mojang)
Good: Creative, educational, no predatory monetization in base game. Bad: Multiplayer chat, third-party servers with no moderation, Bedrock marketplace monetization. **Microsoft could make it fully safe. They choose not to because marketplace revenue.**

### Duolingo
Good: Free language learning, gamified. Bad: Dark patterns (streak anxiety, notifications designed to guilt children into opening the app). **Learning shouldn't come with a guilt engine.**

### Twitch (Amazon)
Good: Creative communities, educational streamers. Bad: Minors streaming to adults, chat toxicity, gambling streams (now partially restricted). **Amazon could fix this overnight. They won't because engagement = ad revenue.**

### Among Us
Good: Simple, social, creative gameplay. Bad: Public lobbies with unmoderated chat, used for grooming by predators who enter children's games. **A good game in a bad environment.**

### Cocomelon / Moonbug Entertainment
Good: Educational intent. Bad: Acquired by Candle Media / private equity. Hypnotic, rapid-fire visual patterns designed to maximize screen time. Researchers question whether the pacing harms attention development. **When private equity buys children's content, the children become the product.**

## The Pattern

Every UNSAFE platform shares the same structure as Detroit's charter schools:

| Charter School Pattern | Platform Pattern |
|---|---|
| Management LLC extracts 72.67% | Platform takes 75% (Roblox) |
| Board chair is a sitting judge | Board members are former regulators |
| 3% math proficiency | No meaningful educational outcome |
| $4.9M public money | Billions in child-generated attention |
| State gave him a second school | FTC fines are < 0.1% of revenue |
| Children can't leave | Addictive design makes leaving painful |

**The extraction is the same. The scale is different.**

Brian Banks takes $4.9M/year from 362 children.
Roblox takes $4.89B/year from 70 million children.

The model scales. The harm scales. The accountability doesn't.
