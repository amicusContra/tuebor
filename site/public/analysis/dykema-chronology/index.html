The Dykema Chronology — 1988 to Present

October 9, 2026

How to Read This

Every entry has a date, a source, and the actors/entities involved. The timeline traces one law firm’s connections across 36 years — from shelf corporations in 1988 to charter school board governance in 2026.

This is not a conspiracy theory. It is a public record.

Phase 1: Building the Legal Framework (1982–1997)

1982 — McLellan Joins Dykema

Richard D. McLellan’s private practice merges with Dykema Gossett. He becomes head of the Government Policy & Practice Group and Managing Member of the Lansing office. He had previously served on Gov. William Milliken’s staff.

Source: Wikipedia; McLellan biography, Mackinac Center.

Actor: Richard McLellan (Dykema partner, Lansing)

1987 — McLellan Co-Founds Mackinac Center

McLellan co-founds the Mackinac Center for Public Policy, a free-market think tank that will become the intellectual engine behind Michigan’s charter school movement. He serves as board secretary for decades.

Source: Mackinac Center

Actors: Richard McLellan, Joseph Olson

1988 — Dykema Registers Shelf Nonprofits

Dykema registers pre-formed nonprofit entities — empty corporate shells ready for activation:

Filed by Cindy M. Wilder at 800 Michigan National Tower, Lansing (Dykema’s former Lansing office). Dissolved within months.

Source: Michigan LARA Business Entity Search

Entity: Dykema Gossett PLLC

1990 — McLellan Runs Engler Transition

McLellan serves as transition director for Governor-elect John Engler. This positions Dykema at the center of the incoming administration that will create charter schools.

Source: Wikipedia; Mackinac Center biography

Actors: Richard McLellan, John Engler

Oct 5, 1993 — Engler Proposes Charter Schools

Governor Engler proposes charter school legislation in a speech before a joint session of the Michigan Legislature.

Source: Founders Library

Actor: John Engler

Dec 3, 1993 — Dykema Writes the Charter School Law

SB 896, creating Part 6a of the Revised School Code, passes the Legislature. Richard McLellan and Leonard Wolfe of Dykema Gossett are the “leading legal architects” of the legislation.

McLellan personally conducted “12 hours of shuttle diplomacy” in the Senate Majority Leader’s conference room to negotiate a version that would survive constitutional challenge.

The law allows:

  • State universities, community colleges, ISDs, and school districts to authorize charter schools
  • For-profit management companies to operate charter schools
  • A 3% authorization fee (incentivizing authorizers to keep schools open)
  • No statutory authorizer evaluation system
  • Board members appointed without identity disclosure requirements

Source: Founders Library; MI Good Government; Dykema.com Education page

Actors: Richard McLellan, Leonard Wolfe (Dykema)

Jan 14, 1994 — Engler Signs PA 362

Governor Engler signs the charter school law. Michigan becomes the 9th state to enact a charter school law.

Source: Founders Library

Actors: John Engler, Richard McLellan, Leonard Wolfe

1994 — First Charter Schools Open

Central Michigan University becomes the first authorizer. Three schools open: Casa Maria Academy (Detroit), New Branches School (Grand Rapids), Northlane Math and Science Academy.

Source: CMU Center for Charter Schools

1997 — Dykema Defends the Law at Supreme Court

The Michigan Education Association challenges the constitutionality of PA 362. Dykema’s McLellan and Wolfe defend the law before the Michigan Supreme Court in Council of Organizations and Affiliates v. Engler, 455 Mich 557 (1997).

The Court upholds the law and orders state funding restored.

Court record lists: “Dykema, Gossett, P.L.L.C. by Richard D. McLellan, Lori M. Silsbury, and Leonard C. Wolfe, Lansing, for intervening Defendants-Appellants.”

Source: Justia; 455 Mich 557

Actors: Richard McLellan, Leonard Wolfe, Lori Silsbury (Dykema)

Phase 2: The Machine Operator Arrives (2009–2016)

2009 — Wilk Forms Larry Deshazor Community Fund

W. Alan Wilk begins his entity formation practice. First documented entity:

EntityLARA IDYear
Larry Deshazor Community Fund70654L2009

Source: Michigan LARA Business Entity Search

Actor: W. Alan Wilk (Dykema)

2011 — NERD Fund

Wilk serves as legal and compliance officer for Governor Snyder’s NERD Fund (New Energy to Reinvent and Diversify). The fund:

  • Raised $2.19M from undisclosed donors over 3 years
  • Paid Rich Baird’s $100K salary
  • Paid EM Kevyn Orr’s travel expenses
  • Shut down in 2013 after transparency criticism

Source: Detroit News, Nov 19, 2014

Actors: W. Alan Wilk (Dykema), Rick Snyder, Rich Baird, Kevyn Orr

2011 — Wolfe Lifts the Charter Cap

Leonard Wolfe “plays instrumental role” in SB 618 (signed Dec 20, 2011), which phases out the cap on university-authorized charter schools. Wolfe attends the signing ceremony with Governor Snyder.

This removes the 150-school limit that had constrained charter expansion since 1999.

Source: Dykema.com

Actors: Leonard Wolfe (Dykema), Rick Snyder

2012 — Banks Elected to State House; Wells-Stallworth Joins MacDowell Board

Brian Roderick Banks elected to the Michigan House of Representatives (D-Detroit). Serves as Chair of the Detroit Caucus.

Nicole Wells Stallworth appointed to MacDowell Preparatory Academy board. She will serve continuously for 14 years.

Source: purposecharteracademy.com; charterschools.org

Actors: Brian Banks, Nicole Wells Stallworth

2015 — Dykema Sues Ellison (Garcia v. Title Check)

Dykema represents the plaintiff in Garcia v. Title Check LLC (1:15-cv-00975, W.D. Michigan). Obtains $73,752.45 in sanctions against attorney Philip L. Ellison for litigation misconduct.

Banks and Ellison were MSU Law classmates (2010).

Source: PACER, W.D. Michigan

Actors: Philip Ellison, Dykema attorneys (unnamed)

Phase 3: Dark Money Pipeline Activates (2016–2021)

Sep 8, 2016 — Wilk Forms Save Detroit Jobs

W. Alan Wilk incorporates Save Detroit Jobs (LARA 802002459). Entity email: compliance@dykema.com. Same day, Wilk also incorporates Detroit Jobs First (per Metro Times, FCC filings).

SDJ board: Rev. James Holley (president, former Duggan civic liaison), Mike Allen (LIUNA Local 1191), Lisa Canada (MI Regional Council of Carpenters).

Both entities are created to fight Proposal A — a citizen-led community benefits ordinance in Detroit.

Source: Michigan LARA; Metro Times

Actors: W. Alan Wilk, James Holley, Mike Allen, Lisa Canada

Nov 2016 — SDJ Pays Carol Banks $14,279; Pays Dykema $9,000

Save Detroit Jobs pays Carol Banks (chief of staff to Councilman Scott Benson) approximately $14,279 via ~70 separate reimbursements.

SDJ also pays Dykema Gossett nearly $9,000 in legal fees.

SDJ raises over $100,000 from: Blue Cross Blue Shield, Turn Around Wayne County PAC (Matthew Moroun’s Central Transport), Schostak Family PAC (Republican). Renae Moore is recordkeeper for Turn Around Wayne County PAC.

Source: Free Press, Sep 22, 2021

Actors: Carol Banks, Scott Benson, W. Alan Wilk, Renae Moore

2017 — Banks Resigns from State House

Banks resigns from the Michigan House as part of a plea deal that allows him to avoid up to 14 years in prison for defrauding a credit union. He is convicted of 8 felonies (later updated to 9 total: 6 felony, 3 misdemeanor).

Source: Free Press, Aug 27, 2023

Actor: Brian Banks

Sep 2018 — Impact Detroit Assumed Name

SDJ registers assumed name “Impact Detroit” (LARA record). First persona change.

Source: Michigan LARA, Entity 802002459

2020 — Wilk Forms Our Neighborhoods First

Wilk incorporates Our Neighborhoods First (LARA 802493189). Officers include Keenann Knox (Impact Church), Lorna Thomas (Public Lighting Authority chair, Duggan appointee), Betty Brooks (ex-police commissioner, “dear friend” of Duggan for 30 years).

Renae Moore serves as both Dykema employee AND compliance officer for the Duggan campaign AND recordkeeper for ONF.

Source: Deadline Detroit

Actors: W. Alan Wilk, Keenann Knox, Renae Moore, Lorna Thomas, Betty Brooks

Aug 10, 2021 — Detroit Leaders Assumed Name

SDJ registers assumed name “Detroit Leaders” — one week after Latisha Johnson and Steve Elrick advance to the general election in District 4. Detroit Leaders sends mailers supporting Johnson against Elrick.

Source: Deadline Detroit

Actors: Latisha Johnson, Steve Elrick, Keenann Knox, W. Alan Wilk

Aug 25, 2021 — FBI Raids Carol Banks

FBI raids Carol Banks’ East English Village home as part of “Operation Northern Hook” — the public corruption investigation that produced Councilman Andre Spivey’s bribery conviction. Agents seek records on 501(c)(4) organizations and campaign financing.

Source: Free Press, Aug 25, 2021

Actor: Carol Banks

Nov 2021 — Johnson Wins, Elrick Loses

Latisha Johnson defeats journalist Steve Elrick in Detroit District 4. The dark money entity formed by Dykema’s Wilk helped defeat the journalist who covered Banks’s network.

Source: Wayne County election results

Actors: Latisha Johnson, Steve Elrick

Phase 4: The Board Is Captured (2022–2025)

Aug 2022 — Banks Applies for School Administrator Certificate

Ten days after losing his county commissioner race, Banks applies for a school administrator certificate from MDE. Granted the next day (Sep 2, 2022 — school starts Aug 29).

Source: Free Press, Aug 27, 2023; MDE records

Actor: Brian Banks

Feb 2022 — MDE Investigates Banks’s Credentials

MDE sends letter to Wells-Stallworth (MacDowell Board Chair) warning that Banks’s employment “may be in violation of the State School Aid Act.” Banks has no valid Michigan teaching credential.

MDE gives the school a procedural loophole rather than enforcing compliance.

Source: MDE FOIA response, Sep 25, 2026

Actors: Brian Banks, Nicole Wells Stallworth, MDE

Jun 2023 — Wells-Stallworth Defends Charter Board Governance

Wells-Stallworth publishes Bridge Michigan op-ed: “Michigan charter school boards ensure financial accountability.” She presides over a board approving 72.67% revenue extraction to Banks’s management LLC.

Appears on MAPSA podcast: “A Charter School Board Member Tells All.”

Source: Bridge Michigan, Jun 9, 2023

Actor: Nicole Wells Stallworth

Jun 2023 — Wilk Forms Put Progress First

Wilk incorporates Put Progress First — a nonprofit backing Duggan’s leadership, signaling a possible gubernatorial bid. Same Dykema formation pattern.

Source: Bridge Detroit

Actors: W. Alan Wilk, Mike Duggan

Aug 2023 — Free Press “Back to Fool”

M.L. Elrick publishes “Back to Fool” — exposing Banks as an 8-time felon hired as principal of MacDowell. Board members had donated to Banks’s 2022 county commissioner campaign before hiring him.

Source: Free Press, Aug 27, 2023

Actor: M.L. Elrick, Brian Banks

Nov 2024 — Purpose Group LLC Filed

The Purpose Group, LLC (LARA 803295082) filed November 14, 2024. Sole member: Brian Banks. This is the management company that will receive 72.67% of school revenue.

Source: Michigan LARA

Actors: Brian Banks

Dec 2024 — Michigan Charter Reform Bills

Michigan legislature passes charter reform requiring authorizers to review contracts over $100K, attend 50%+ of board meetings, and mandate audited EMO financials. This legislation is directly responsive to governance failures like Banks’s operation.

Source: Chalkbeat, Dec 13, 2024

Jan 2025 — FBI Closes Carol Banks Investigation

FBI closes the public corruption investigation into Scott Benson and Janée Ayers without charges. Carol Banks is not charged.

Source: Free Press, Jan 17, 2025

Actor: Carol Banks
