## What This Means

You enrolled your child in a school. You trusted the name on the building. You trusted that someone — the state, the district, the board — checked.

They didn't. Or they did, and authorized it anyway. DPSCD takes a 3% fee — about $113,000 per year — for authorizing the charter. The charter runs through June 2027.

You have rights. Michigan law gives parents the right to:

- **Request school records** via FOIA (Public Act 442 of 1976)
- **Attend board meetings** and speak during public comment
- **Contact the authorizer** (DPSCD) about charter compliance
- **File complaints** with the Michigan Department of Education
- **Transfer your child** to any school in the district

A guide to FOIA for Michigan school parents is at [Clutch Justice](https://clutchjustice.com/2026/09/23/foia-michigan-school-board-records-guide/).
