Rita's broader investigation into how Detroit institutions fail at the most
basic function: making and keeping records that allow accountability.

## Why This Matters

This evidence library exists because the records *do* exist — in OTIS, LARA,
campaign finance databases, court dockets, property records. The problem isn't
that the evidence is hidden. The problem is that no institution connected the
dots. That's the gap this site fills.

Clutch's "Detroit Files" series examines the institutional culture that makes
this gap possible. This evidence library is the practical demonstration: what
happens when someone actually pulls all the records together.
