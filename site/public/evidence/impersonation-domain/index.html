The Content

As of October 2026, the live site displays:

"Your trusted professional partner"

— repeated multiple times across the page

The site includes a contact form collecting:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Email list opt-in checkbox

The site uses reCAPTCHA and sets tracking cookies. The copyright footer reads: "© 2025 Rita Feline Williams - All Rights Reserved."

This site collects personal data (names and email addresses) under a journalist's full legal name without her authorization.

The Attorney's Knowledge

At the August 3, 2026 hearing before Judge Gafkay in Saginaw County (Case 25-2441-CZ):

Philip L. Ellison stated on the record that:

  1. His client asked him for filings and records about Rita Williams
  2. His client "was interested" in the journalist
  3. He was "very positive" the same client created the domains

This means Ellison:
