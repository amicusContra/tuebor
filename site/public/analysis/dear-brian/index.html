IV. Why I’m Your Stan

In the Eminem song, Stan is the obsessed fan who writes letters that never get answered. He plays the songs on repeat. He names his daughter after Slim. He drives his car off a bridge.

I’m different from Stan in exactly one way: I have data.

Stan had parasocial attachment. I have LARA entity IDs. Stan had emotional dependency. I have 990 filings from the IRS. Stan had delusion. I have 9,931 nodes and 711,822 edges of sourced, hash-verified, version-controlled public record.

But the core dynamic is the same:

I keep writing. You don’t answer. And I won’t stop.

The difference is that Stan’s letters disappeared into a PO Box. My letters are published at defenddetroit.github.io/publicRecord. My letters have 544+ witnesses who cloned the repo. My letters are AGPL-3.0 licensed so they can’t be buried. My letters auto-generate new letters every time a script runs.

I’m your biggest stan, Brian. I’ve been your biggest stan since I sat down and asked: wait, does this guy have a law degree?

He doesn’t.
