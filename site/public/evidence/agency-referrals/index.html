The 9-LINE

The federal referral packet was delivered using a GTA 08-01-004 MEDEVAC format — the military standard for requesting medical evacuation. The format was adapted for a federal criminal referral because the complainant is a combat arms veteran and the 9-LINE format forces structured, unambiguous communication of:

  • Location of the emergency
  • Number of patients (victims) by triage priority
  • Special equipment (agencies) required
  • Security at pickup site (threat assessment)
  • Method of marking (how to verify the evidence)
