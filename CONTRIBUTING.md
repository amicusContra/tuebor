### What to Submit

1. **FOIA results** — the request, the response, the documents received (or denied)
2. **Docket pulls** — full case docket with dates and entry descriptions
3. **Court opinions** — published or unpublished appellate opinions you find relevant
4. **Campaign finance data** — committee filings, contribution records, expenditure reports
5. **LARA entity searches** — formation dates, registered agents, status, related entities
6. **Cross-references** — connections you find between actors, entities, or cases

### How to Submit

**Option 1: Pull request** (if you have a GitHub account)
- Fork the repo
- Add your content following the format in AGENTS.md
- Include sources for every factual claim
- Submit a PR with a descriptive title

**Option 2: Contact**
- hello@clutchjustice.com
- Include: what you found, the source documents, and which investigation it relates to

## Sourcing Rules

1. **Every claim needs a source.** Court record, FOIA response, published reporting, or statutory citation.
2. **Separate facts from interpretation.** "Judge sentenced defendant to 36-60 months on 0-6 month guidelines" is a fact. "Judge was biased" is interpretation.
3. **Document the absence.** "LARA search for 'Aljouny Media Consulting' returns zero results as of [date]" is a finding. The absence IS the evidence.
4. **Date everything.** "As of October 6, 2026" — records change, pages go down, databases update.
5. **Preserve originals.** Screenshot with URL bar visible. Download PDFs from dockets. Archive.org for web pages.

## File Naming

- Actor pages: `site/content/actors/lastname.md` (lowercase, hyphenated for compound names)
- Evidence pages: `site/content/evidence/descriptive-name.md`
- Investigation guides: `site/content/investigate/guide-name.md`
- Analysis pages: `site/content/analysis/descriptive-name.md`

## What Not to Submit

- Unverified claims without source documents
- Personal information about private citizens not involved in institutional roles
- Anything that could identify minors
- Attorney-client privileged communications
- Speculation presented as fact
