## The Statewide Architecture of Failure

### Why It's Not Corruption — It's Design

The word "corruption" implies individual bad actors making deliberate choices to subvert the system. That framing misses what's actually happening.

Michigan's oversight architecture was **designed** to process complaints in isolation:
- AGC complaint intake does not cross-reference complainants across cases
- JTC proceedings are confidential by design
- SCAO systemic reviews are discretionary
- LARA entity searches require someone to know what to search for

No single design choice is unreasonable in isolation. Together, they create a system that **cannot detect patterns.** Not because it chooses not to — because it was not built to.

### The 94% Dismissal Rate

The AGC dismisses approximately 94% of complaints. This is not a statistic about frivolous complaints. It is a **structural throughput rate.**

If you are the subject of a complaint, you have a 94% chance of no public consequence. If you are the subject of complaints in three different investigations processed in isolation, your chances are 0.94³ = 83%. The isolation multiplies protection.

If the investigations were cross-referenced — if the AGC saw that the same attorney appeared in complaints from multiple people across multiple counties — the probability calculation changes. Pattern evidence is qualitatively different from individual complaints.

**The system does not cross-reference.** That is the design flaw this site exists to address.

### Anderson Localization — Multi-Scale

The [Detroit investigation](https://detroit.primals.eco/analysis/anderson-localization/) validated Anderson localization at N=620,000 (metropolitan scale). The [Hemlock subgraph](/analysis/anderson-hemlock/) validated it at N=2,000 (small-town scale).

The localization operates at both scales because the **same oversight bodies** serve both. A complaint from Detroit and a complaint from Hemlock reach the same AGC, the same JTC, the same SCAO. The processing is identical. The isolation is identical. The failure mode is identical.

The physics scales because the lattice structure — the institutional architecture — is statewide. County boundaries change the surface details. They do not change the wave propagation dynamics.
