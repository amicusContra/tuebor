External Coverage

Independent reporters and investigative projects have covered the same people, institutions, and systemic failures documented in this evidence library. This section cross-references their work with ours.

We are not the source of these stories. The reporters are. We are the index — the place where the threads converge.

How This Works

Each entry links to an independent article and maps it to pages in this evidence library that cover the same ground. If you came here from one of these articles, the cross-references show you what else the public record reveals. If you came here first, these articles provide independent verification and context.

Signal Categories

We cross-index external coverage in three categories:

  1. Direct overlap — articles about the same people and entities we document
  2. Structural context — articles about the Michigan systems (courts, authorizers, oversight) that enable the patterns we document
  3. Methodological — articles about how public records work, how to obtain them, and what institutional record-keeping reveals

Every fact is cited. Every document is timestamped.
