Active JTC Investigation

Sources with direct knowledge of Judicial Tenure Commission proceedings confirm Schipper is under active JTC investigation. The inquiry covers at least four documented areas:

  1. "Man made" sentencing doctrine — on-record position that sentencing guidelines are discretionary because they are "man made"
  2. Unfinalized data in sentencing — use of unfinalized data in sentencing decisions
  3. Media access policy — excludes every outlet except one preferred publication while waiving compliance requirements for that outlet
  4. ADA retaliation — ordered mental competency examination against defendant Jeffrey Snowden immediately after Snowden asserted rights under the Americans with Disabilities Act

Source: Clutch Justice, Apr 25, 2026

People v. Velasquez

Schipper sentenced a defendant with guidelines of 0–6 months to 36–60 months — six to ten times the top of the guideline range.

  • Assistant Prosecutor Christopher Elsworth filed a Confession of Error, formally conceding the plea agreement had been broken
  • Michigan Court of Appeals vacated the sentence
  • On remand, Schipper imposed a second sentence still outside the guidelines
  • The Court of Appeals vacated it again and took the unusual step of barring Schipper from presiding over the resentencing
  • A different judge resentenced the defendant to time served (April 2025)

Source: Clutch Justice, Sep 25, 2026

The Snowden Case

Jeffrey Snowden is an Iraqi War veteran and former Barry County resident.

  • Alleged fraudulent arrest
  • Brady and Giglio violations alleged — exculpatory evidence withheld
  • Asserted rights under the Americans with Disabilities Act
  • Met with a competency motion — Schipper ordered mental competency exam after ADA assertion
  • FOIA requests for court and prosecutorial records denied as "too broad"
  • Appeared before Barry County Board of Commissioners (February 2026) and placed the Board on Preservation Notice
  • Has documented the conduct patterns Schipper's court has produced

What Court Watchers Report

People who regularly sit in on Barry County proceedings report these patterns:

  • Decides outcome before hearing argument
  • Pretrial detention used as pressure rather than risk-based decision
  • Inappropriate personal remarks to people appearing before him (including soliciting for outside speaking engagements)
  • Bail withheld to push defendants toward plea

These are reported observations, not independently verified with transcripts. They are consistent with the sentencing pattern the appellate record supports.

Source: Clutch Justice, Sep 25, 2026

SCAO Systemic Review Request

Clutch Justice filed a request for SCAO to conduct a systemic administrative review of Barry County courts — not just individual case appeals. The filing asks SCAO to:

  • Pull hearing recordings and transcripts directly
  • Include Schipper's pretrial and courtroom conduct in its review
  • Review his sentencing record systemically, not just on individual appeal

The federal government has also stepped in — the title of Clutch Justice's reporting: "Clutch Justice Asked the State to Investigate Barry County's Courts. The Feds Have Stepped In."

Source: Clutch Justice, Sep 25, 2026
