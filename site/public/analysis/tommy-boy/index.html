Tommy Boy

Barrett's campaign received $4,000 from Dykema Gossett Federal PAC.

Dykema Gossett incorporated a dark money entity whose president is Kenneth Daniels — a convicted federal felon who structured cash for a $21 million drug ring (DEA press release, May 12, 2014).

The word "felon" travels outward. Actual felons travel inward.

The Amplification Machine

Barrett didn't act alone. The "convicted felon" label moved through a staged pipeline:

Stage 1: NRCC Pre-Staging

The National Republican Congressional Committee's democratfacts.org pre-staged "convicted felon" opposition research on Lawrence in 2022 — before Lawrence announced his candidacy AND before Michigan's automatic expungement provisions took effect.

When Barrett deployed the label in August 2026, the infrastructure was already built.

NRCC spokesman Zach Bannon: "Socialist felon William Lawrence is a radical far-left liberal who puts his extreme climate agenda ahead of common sense and the priorities of hardworking Michiganders." (WWMT)

Stage 2: Jason Roe ($782,000)

Barrett's campaign strategist Jason Roe — Principal of Roe Strategic LLC, former Michigan Republican Party Executive Director — was paid $782,000 by the Barrett campaign (FEC disbursement records, Committee C00793976).

Roe's own website boasts: "2024: Strategist for Tom Barrett's victory in Michigan's 7th Congressional District, one of the most expensive and competitive seats in the nation."

$782,000 to one consultant. The Tenant Resource Center of Mid Michigan's annual budget from the Ingham County Housing Trust Fund is $600,000.

Stage 3: Right-Wing Media Cascade

Following Barrett's remarks, right-wing outlets escalated by targeting individual Rent Is Too Damn High volunteers — publishing personal details, home addresses, and criminal histories. Coalition internal records were leaked and weaponized.

The message to any prospective volunteer with any criminal history: your past will be weaponized against the movement if you participate.

Stage 4: Chilling Effect

Specific individuals lost their capacity to volunteer with the tenant advocacy coalition as a direct result of the smear cascade. Their privacy is not disclosed here because — unlike the smear machine — this site does not publish individual humans' personal details to score political points.

The effect is measurable: fewer volunteers, reduced capacity, tenant advocacy organizations spending time defending their people instead of defending tenants.
