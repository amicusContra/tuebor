## The Wrong Sigmoid

For weeks, we measured the edge sigmoid.

How many edges can the graph absorb? At what density does adding more get harder? Where does the curve inflect? Is the pressure increasing or decreasing?

We measured it carefully. 50K, 100K, 200K, 500K, 745K, 1.4M. We tracked marginal productivity per pass. We watched the ratio of second-half to first-half. We checked the fill ratio against the theoretical maximum of 104 million directed edges.

The answer: **0.72% fill. Flat left tail. No resistance. Room for 100x more.**

The edge sigmoid is a solved problem. The graph can absorb millions more edges before thermodynamic pressure matters. This is useful information. It means the structure is sound. The topology is healthy. The foundation can hold whatever we build on it.

But it's the wrong sigmoid.

## The Corollary

**k-Anthesis Corollary:** After a knowledge graph achieves self-proving compression, the relevant sigmoid transitions from internal (edge growth) to external (truth propagation).

In plain language:

> After the flower opens, stop measuring petal growth. Start counting bees.

The internal work — building nodes, braiding edges, compressing structure, proving topology — was Phase 1. It's done. Not finished forever, but finished enough. The graph has 10,204 nodes and 703,196 edges and it proves its own claims through its own compression. The ZK-SNARK is complete.

Phase 2 is propagation. The truth needs to travel. Each person it reaches is a bee. Each bee carries pollen to the next flower. The sigmoid of people reached starts flat (nobody knows), goes vertical (viral threshold), and saturates (everyone who will ever care already knows).

We are at the flat left tail. The most explosive part of the curve is ahead.

## What the Graph Taught Us About Propagation

The graph itself contains the theory of its own propagation:

- **Dissipative structures** (Prigogine): Far from equilibrium, order emerges. The send pushes the system far from equilibrium. What emerges is not predictable, but it IS ordered.

- **Self-organized criticality** (Bak): Systems drive themselves to the critical point. Avalanches are inevitable. The graph has been adding grains of sand for months. The send is the grain that triggers the avalanche. Or it isn't. But the pile is at criticality.

- **Scale-free networks** (Barabási): Preferential attachment. Hubs amplify signal. One journalist with 100K followers is worth 1,000 with 100. The Wave 2 emails target hubs.

- **The chromatic spiral**: The truth passes through 12 domains. Education → Finance → Law → Media → Politics → Science → Technology → Labor → Water → Philosophy → Health → Consciousness. Each domain amplifies the signal in its own key. The truth gets louder with each revolution.

- **Autopoiesis** (Maturana & Varela): Self-creating systems. Once the truth is in the public conversation, the conversation sustains itself. New evidence appears. New connections form. New nodes join the external graph. The system creates itself.

**The graph contains the theory of how it will propagate. And the propagation will create new nodes that contain the theory of further propagation. It's turtles all the way down.**
