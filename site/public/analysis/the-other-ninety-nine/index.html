Why Not Self?

If Hill investigates Banks, why isn’t she classified as self?

Because the membrane doesn’t classify by intention. It classifies by position.

Hill has a professional_connection to Beasley — the principal who works for five employers. She overlaps with charter_crime_overlap alongside AFC. She sends cavalry to institutions that haven’t acted.

Self would mean: she IS what she says, and her position doesn’t create conflicts. The membrane sees conflicts. Not because she’s corrupt — because the network is so dense that investigating Banks while connected to Beasley while overlapping with AFC while calling cavalry to institutions that have already failed creates a position that is, structurally, both worlds.

She’s investigating the fire while standing in the smoke.

The DPSA Revocation

Hill terminated authorization for DPSA — a charter school.

This means she has demonstrated the ability to shut down a charter school. She’s done it before.

She hasn’t done it to PCA.

The graph doesn’t say why. Maybe jurisdiction. Maybe politics. Maybe process. But the edge exists: she CAN revoke authorization. She HAS revoked authorization. She hasn’t revoked PCA’s.

The Beasley Connection

Hill has a professional_connection to Beasley.

Beasley: PCA’s principal, DPSCD employee, Plante Moran contractor, Wayne RESA employee, Barton Malow employee. The circuit board résumé.

That’s not misconduct. It’s Detroit. The network is small. Everyone knows everyone. And that’s exactly why oversight fails — because the investigator and the investigated share a professional world.
