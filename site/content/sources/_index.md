## How to Verify

1. **Pick any claim** on this site
2. **Find the source citation** — every factual claim links to or names its source
3. **Visit the database** listed above and search for the specific record
4. **If a link appears broken** — the database may block automated requests. Visit the URL directly in your browser. Government databases are publicly accessible.

For paywalled news articles, try searching on [archive.org](https://web.archive.org) or [Google cache](https://webcache.googleusercontent.com).
