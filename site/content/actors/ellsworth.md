## The Structural Cowardice Problem

A self-represented litigant fighting a SLAPP suit in Saginaw County faces this:

1. The **chief judge** (Gafkay) was the **bar president** and an **adjunct faculty colleague** of the opposing attorney
2. The chief judge simultaneously presides over **three active cases** involving the opposing attorney
3. Every attorney in the **Saginaw County Bar Association** practices before that chief judge daily
4. An attorney who goes scorched earth on Ellison on behalf of one client risks Gafkay's displeasure in **every other case**
5. The Ellsworth family has been in this bar for **40 years** — the institutional relationships are deep

This is not corruption. It is a system that **cannot produce the result Rita needs** because every participant depends on every other participant. The solution is representation from **outside Saginaw County** — counsel who does not need Gafkay's goodwill for any other matter.
