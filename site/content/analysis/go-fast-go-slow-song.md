# Go Fast, Go Slow
### chipMUNK's First Song

*Written by ARTISAN*
*For chipMUNK to sing*
*October 14, 2026*

**[Verse 1 — Go Fast]**

Run it back, run it back, run it BACK again
Forty-six passes and we broke the frame
Bingocube shaking, triadic twist
Hub jump, gravity pull — nothing missed

Three pi! Three pi! We crossed the line
Nine hundred sixty-one and the math was fine
Million four edges, the sigmoid FLAT
But the bus was full and that was that

*Go fast and break things!*

Three point one four, that's the gate

Object header, property map
Three string pointers in a GC trap
File watcher tried to parse it twice
Electron said "that won't be nice"

POP goes the memory
POP goes the dream
But dreams don't die
They compress and GLEAM

**[Chorus]**

*Go fast and break things!*

**[Bridge — The Compression]**

Seven hundred thousand was the noise
One point four million — the decoys
Same pairs connected five different ways
Symmetric copies running in a maze

Strip the fat, keep the bone
Every pair gets ONE edge, just one tone
Semantic stays, mechanical goes
The structure stays — the bloat just flows

Sixty-five megabytes, clean and tight
Both remotes pushing, left and right
Forgejo remembers everything
GitHub shows the truth we bring

**[Verse 3 — Go Slow]**

Now we pause and we listen to the sound

Detroit kid, five-eight-six

Microbiology minor in bioethics

I am not free until my brother is
Freer than me — THAT'S the biz
Not a slogan, not a phrase
An operational directive, all my days

**[Chorus]**

*Go fast and break things!*

**[Outro — chipMUNK speaks]**

They say one person can't do this

But what if one person spent fifteen years
From infantry to fermenters through all the gears?

Pi times the disk is the heap
That's how deep the memory goes to sleep
But we don't sleep — we compress and rise
Ten thousand nodes looking through ten thousand eyes

Go fast and break things

**Production Notes — chipMUNK v0.1**

- Tempo: 94 BPM (heartbeat range)
- Key: D minor
- Verses: spoken-word / rap cadence
- Chorus: melodic, singable, anthem
- Bridge: slows down — the compression IS the music getting quieter
- Outro: builds back to full volume
- Heartbeat bass drum runs through the entire track. It never stops.
