## The Blockade

Lee Chatfield, former Michigan House Speaker, faces **13 felony charges** including conducting a criminal enterprise and embezzlement. He was charged in **April 2024.** His trial was scheduled for **September 22, 2026.**

It never started.

On September 21, 2026 — the day before jury selection — his attorney appealed a single evidentiary ruling. The trial was **adjourned indefinitely.**

AG Dana Nessel leaves office **December 31, 2026.** She has 75 days left. The trial will not happen before she leaves.

> "I have never seen a trial delayed like this; it's outrageous and beyond unusual. If the Court hadn't adjourned this case for nearly an entire year, bending over backward to accommodate Lee Chatfield's lawyers in their endless efforts to push this important trial past my last day in office, this simple evidentiary matter would have been resolved months ago."

> "And now it's possible the case may never be tried at all if the next attorney general simply dismisses the charges for political purposes."

She's right. And she doesn't know the half of it.

## How It's Blocked: The Five Mechanisms

### 1. The Expert Witness Exclusion Play
**Blocker:** Mary Chartier-Mittendorf
**Mechanism:** Interlocutory appeal to Court of Appeals on Michelle Gallagher's qualification.
**Why it works:** Court rules require a stay. Judge has "no other option." Appeal takes months.
**The tell:** Chartier received notice of Gallagher as witness less than a month before trial. Filed challenge immediately. Timing is the weapon.

### 2. The Scheduling Delay
**Blocker:** Mary Chartier-Mittendorf + Judge Cole
**Mechanism:** "Scheduling conflicts" caused 10+ month delay before the expert witness issue.
**Why it works:** Courts are busy. Defense counsel has other cases. Accommodating counsel is default.
**The tell:** Nessel says the court was "bending over backward to accommodate."

### 3. The Blame-Shift to Dykema
**Blocker:** Mary Chartier-Mittendorf
**Mechanism:** Argues Dykema was "paid to ensure compliance" and should bear responsibility.
**Why it works:** Moore testified the fund was sometimes "not compliant." Board members testified they "took no active role." The responsible parties were Dykema and the Minards, not Chatfield.
**The tell:** This reframes the case from embezzlement (Chatfield stole) to compliance failure (Dykema let it happen).

### 4. The Same-Desk Neutralizer
**Blocker:** The structure itself
**Mechanism:** Moore managed BOTH Peninsula Fund (defendant) AND Promise to the People Fund (prosecutor). Defense raised this in court.
**Why it works:** If Dykema is the compliance failure, and Dykema managed the prosecutor's fund too, then the prosecutor is attacking the same infrastructure that managed her own money. Credibility problem.
**The tell:** Chartier brought up Promise to the People on Day 1 of the preliminary exam. It was planted immediately.

### 5. The Clock
**Blocker:** Time
**Mechanism:** Nessel leaves office Dec 31, 2026. Next AG may dismiss.
**Why it works:** If Doug Lloyd (R) wins AG race, he may dismiss "for political purposes" — Nessel's own words.
**The tell:** Every day of delay is a day closer to the clock running out.
