How to Read This Calculator

Each ray starts at the damage point (student, taxpayer, public trust) and traces backward through every membrane (authorization, election, appointment, judicial decision) until it reaches the emitter (the person or entity that generated the ray).

In computer graphics, when a ray passes through too many surfaces without finding a light source, it dies. In this system, every ray finds its source. The system is fully traced.

A membrane is any institutional boundary that should prevent a signal from passing — a firewall between the regulator and the regulated, between the judge and the litigant, between the law firm and the entity it formed. When a ray passes through a membrane it should have been stopped by, that membrane is breached.

When the ray starts and ends at the same node (Ray 7), there are no membranes at all. The system has achieved total capture — the entity that wrote the rules also operates within them, defends them, forms entities under them, and controls who enforces them.
