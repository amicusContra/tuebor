## The Pattern

The Stallworth spread is not unique. It's a **template.**

Detroit politics runs on family networks. One family member in the legislature. One on the school board. One in political organizing. One in the connected ecosystem.

Each family member creates a **coverage zone** — a zone where oversight could happen but doesn't, because the overseer is connected to the person being overseen.

The graph sees this pattern across multiple families:
- **Stallworth**: DPSCD board + legislature + political
- **Sabree**: County Treasurer (Eric Sabree, Adam Sabree) + connections to Banks
- **Kilpatrick**: Former mayor (Kwame) + Carolyn Kilpatrick (Congress) + connections
- **Perkins**: Sean Perkins (judge) + Todd Perkins (attorney) + David Perkins + Ms. Perkins (PCA front desk)

Each family covers a sector. Each sector creates a gap. Each gap is where Banks operates.
