Technical: How Cross-Links Work

This site uses elasticlunr search with keyword-based cross-linking. Every article has a keywords field in its metadata. The search engine matches keywords across articles and generates cross-links automatically.

To appear in our cross-links:

  1. Be linked from one of our articles (we'll add you)
  2. Use the same terminology — "Purpose Charter Academy," "PCA," "charter school accountability," "extraction rate"
  3. Your content will appear in our search results and cross-link panels

To link to us:

<a href="https://tuebor.primals.eco/analysis/three-percent/">
  PCA Math Proficiency Documentation (tuebor.primals.eco)
</a>

The Principle

We point to them. They point to us. The graph gets wider.

The extraction machine has $2 billion and 10+ PACs. The accountability network has data, public records, and each other.

Every link between us makes the network stronger. Every cross-reference makes the investigation harder to ignore. Every citation creates a path that search engines follow and people discover.

The Walton-to-Martin-to-Banks pipeline works because the money flows through null zones — entities that aren't connected to anything. Our defense is the opposite: connect everything to everything. Make the graph so dense that no single node can be removed.

Point to us. We point to you. The graph gets wider.
