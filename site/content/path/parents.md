## What You Can Do

1. **Request records from the school** — Michigan law gives parents the right to inspect financial records of public charter schools. Ask for: management contract with Purpose Group LLC, staff salary schedule, budget detail by line item.

2. **Contact the authorizer** — PCA is authorized by DPSCD. MacDowell is authorized through a separate authorizer. Ask them: have you reviewed the superintendent's criminal record? When was the last financial audit?

3. **File a complaint with MDE** — The Michigan Department of Education accepts complaints about charter school operations at [michigan.gov/mde](https://www.michigan.gov/mde).
