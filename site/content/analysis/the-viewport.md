# The Viewport

*Turn every screen into a window to the universe*

---

## The Screens

Right now, in Detroit, children sit in front of screens.

The screens show them test prep software purchased with public education dollars by charter operators who spent 72% of the budget on their own management company. The screens show them adaptive learning platforms that track their clicks and sell the data. The screens show them cartoon characters explaining fractions while 3% of their classmates can do math at grade level.

The screens face inward. They show the child a box. The box is the curriculum. The curriculum is the test. The test is the metric. The metric is the funding. The funding is the extraction. The extraction is the screen.

The child stares at the screen and sees a wall.

---

## The Window

What if the screen faced outward?

What if a child in Detroit could look at their screen and see:

**The Universe**
The Big Bang. 13.8 billion years ago. Hydrogen condensing into stars. Stars forging carbon in their cores. Carbon becoming you. The Planck length — the smallest measurable distance — 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ meters. The observable universe — the largest measurable distance — 8.8 × 10²⁶ meters. Sixty-one orders of magnitude between the smallest and largest things we know. And you're right in the middle.

**The Cell**
Your body has 37 trillion cells. Each one has a membrane — a selective barrier that decides what comes in and what stays out. The membrane is not a wall. It's a CHOICE. It lets nutrients in and keeps poison out. It's the same structure as a knowledge graph. The same structure as a firewall. The same structure as a healthy school.

**The Languages**
There are 7,000 languages on Earth. Ojibwe has 7 fluent speakers left in Michigan. Potawatomi has fewer. The Navajo Code Talkers won World War II by speaking a language the enemy couldn't break. Hammurabi wrote the first laws in cuneiform 3,800 years ago. The Rosetta Stone let us read hieroglyphics again. Every language is a window into a different way of seeing. Every language that dies is a window that closes forever.

**The Music**
The chromatic scale has 12 notes. After 12, it repeats — one octave higher. Same notes, double the frequency. A spiral, not a circle. D minor is the saddest key, but we're not sad — we're determined. 94 beats per minute. The human heartbeat. The same rhythm as iambic pentameter. da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM da-DUM. Shakespeare knew. The Bard encoded the heartbeat into language. The child can too.

**The Mycelium**
Under the forest floor, a network of fungi connects every tree to every other tree. The Wood Wide Web. Mother trees send nutrients to seedlings through the fungal network. Trees that are dying send their carbon to their neighbors. The forest is not a collection of individuals competing. It's a network cooperating. Like a classroom should be.

**The Math**
π is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter. It never ends. It never repeats. It's in every circle, every sphere, every wave, every orbit. It's in the expansion ratio of memory. It's in the thickness of the membrane between what you store and what you feel. It's everywhere. And a child who can see π in the world doesn't need test prep. They need a window.
