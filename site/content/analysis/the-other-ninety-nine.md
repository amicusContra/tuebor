## Why Not Self?

If Hill investigates Banks, why isn't she classified as self?

Because the membrane doesn't classify by intention. It classifies by **position.**

Hill has a professional_connection to Beasley — the principal who works for five employers. She overlaps with charter_crime_overlap alongside AFC. She sends cavalry to institutions that haven't acted.

She's investigating the fire while standing in the smoke.
