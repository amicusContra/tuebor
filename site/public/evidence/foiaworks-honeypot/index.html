FOIAworks: A Transparency Tool With No Privacy Policy, Operated by the Attorney Who Fabricated a Witness

Summary

FOIAworks.com is a Michigan-focused FOIA request software platform. It collects user account information, FOIA request content, government correspondence, response documents, and payment data.

It is owned and operated by Quagmire Solutions, LLC — a Hemlock, Michigan LLC whose registered agent is Philip L. Ellison (LARA Entity ID: B0703X, formed June 2, 2005, status: Active).

Philip L. Ellison is the same attorney who:

  • Submitted a fabricated witness ("Samantha Aljouny") in Saginaw County litigation
  • Filed a SLAPP suit against a journalist who investigated that fabrication
  • Whose client registered domains targeting PPO holders, including a minor child
  • Whose office IP address surveilled the journalist's website from LakeNet LLC, the local ISP in Hemlock — the same tiny community where both his law firm and Quagmire Solutions are located

FOIAworks has no published privacy policy. The /privacy/ URL returns 404 Not Found (verified October 6, 2026).

  5. Activists researching government accountability

What the Platform Operator Learns

If someone files a FOIA request through FOIAworks, Quagmire Solutions knows:

  • WHO is investigating (name, email, account)
  • WHAT records they want (request text)
  • WHICH government agency is the target
  • WHETHER the agency complied or denied
  • WHAT the government produced (the actual documents)
  • WHEN deadlines and appeals are due

This is a complete intelligence picture of every active FOIA investigation running through the platform.
