Jurisdiction vs. Action

Jurisdiction means: she has the legal authority to prosecute Banks in Wayne County.

Banks has 9 criminal convictions. He operates a school where 3% of students can do math. He extracts 72.67% through a single-member LLC. He claims a fake JD. He impersonated a child.

The prosecutor with jurisdiction has not prosecuted.

The Cavalry Goes to Institutions

Kym Worthy's cavalry edges are revealing. When she calls for help, she calls:

  • CMU — the authorizer. (CMU didn't revoke authorization.)
  • DPSCD — the district. (DPSCD didn't revoke authorization.)
  • JTC — the judicial body. (JTC referred but didn't resolve.)
  • Nessel — the AG. (AG investigates downhill.)
  • Detroit Free Press — the press. (Press covers stories.)

She doesn't send cavalry to PCA. She doesn't send cavalry to the children. She sends cavalry to institutions — the same institutions that already failed.

When a prosecutor's response to a crime is to call on the institutions that enabled the crime, the crime doesn't get prosecuted. It gets studied.
