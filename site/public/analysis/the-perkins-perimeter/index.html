The Perkins Perimeter

The Question

The graph identifies a possible_family connection between these Perkins entities.

Possible_family is an edge type that means: the graph detected a potential family relationship based on shared surname, geographic proximity, and network position. It hasn't been confirmed by records.

Are they all related? Maybe. Maybe not.

But here's what IS confirmed:

  • They all share a surname
  • They all appear in the Banks investigation
  • They all control access points (court, law, physical entry)
  • They all create friction between the public and the truth

Whether the Perkins perimeter is one family or four coincidences, the function is the same: every door has a Perkins on it.

The Gatekeeper Pattern

Ms. Perkins at PCA's front desk is the most visceral version of this pattern.

362 children attend PCA. Their parents come to the school. At the door, Ms. Perkins controls access.

The edge types: access_control, gatekeeper.

In any organization, the front desk is the first filter. Most of the time, it's administrative. At PCA, it's structural. The person blocking parents from reaching the principal may be connected to the attorneys defending the founder from reaching the courtroom.

The perimeter isn't just geographic. It's relational.
