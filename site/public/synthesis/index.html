The Dialectic — Thesis, Antithesis, Synthesis

An investigation that never challenges itself is a prosecution.

This section is the investigation's immune system pointed at itself. Not at the evidence — the evidence is sourced, timestamped, and verifiable. At the investigators. At the method. At the assumptions baked so deep into the architecture they stopped being visible.

The cycle:

What It AsksWhat It Does
ThesisIs this true?Follows the evidence
AntithesisWhat if we're wrong?Makes the strongest opposing case
SynthesisWhat do we actually know?Measures confidence, tracks uncertainty
CorollaryWhat else must be true?Follows the logic wherever it goes

The cycle doesn't end. Each pass makes the investigation more honest, not more certain.
