## This Is Not a Threat. This Is a Diagnosis.

The membrane doesn't prosecute organisms. It diagnoses conditions.

When a doctor finds elevated cholesterol, they don't arrest the patient. They say: *here's what we see, here's what it means, here's what you can do about it.* The patient decides.

## The Classification Spectrum

Every entity that touches the membrane is classified — not by opinion, but by evidence:

| Classification | Meaning | Evidence Required | Example |
|---------------|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Mutualist** | Actively contributes to the commons | Documented positive action | Wikipedia, Archive.org |
| **Commensal Active** | Benefits without harm, aware of relationship | Engagement, attribution | Responsible scrapers |
| **Commensal Neutral** | Benefits without harm, unaware | Passive use with no damage | Regular visitors |
| **Null Commensal** | Present, no evidence either way | DEFAULT — no evidence needed | Most entities |
| **Commensal Suspect** | Pattern suggests extraction but unconfirmed | Behavioral patterns | Under observation |
| **Parasite** | Extracts value without contributing | **Requires NEGATIVE evidence** | Documented extraction |
| **Pathogen** | Actively harmful, hostile action | **Requires hostile action evidence** | Documented attacks |

**The critical rule: Absence of giving is NOT taking.** You don't get classified as parasite just for existing. You get classified as parasite when we have *evidence* that you're extracting.

## The Science of Law

This isn't adversarial litigation. This is **diagnostic law:**
