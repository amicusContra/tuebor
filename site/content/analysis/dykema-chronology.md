## Phase 1: Building the Legal Framework (1982–1997)

### 1982 — McLellan Joins Dykema

Richard D. McLellan's private practice merges with Dykema Gossett.
He becomes head of the Government Policy & Practice Group and Managing
Member of the Lansing office. He had previously served on Gov. William
Milliken's staff.

**Source:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_D._McLellan);
McLellan biography, Mackinac Center.

**Actor:** Richard McLellan (Dykema partner, Lansing)

---

### 1987 — McLellan Co-Founds Mackinac Center

McLellan co-founds the **Mackinac Center for Public Policy**, a
free-market think tank that will become the intellectual engine
behind Michigan's charter school movement. He serves as board
secretary for decades.

**Source:** [Mackinac Center](https://www.mackinac.org/about/board-of-directors/154)

**Actors:** Richard McLellan, Joseph Olson

**Actor:** John Engler

### Dec 3, 1993 — Dykema Writes the Charter School Law

SB 896, creating Part 6a of the Revised School Code, passes the
Legislature. **Richard McLellan** and **Leonard Wolfe** of Dykema
Gossett are the "leading legal architects" of the legislation.

McLellan personally conducted "12 hours of shuttle diplomacy" in the
Senate Majority Leader's conference room to negotiate a version that
would survive constitutional challenge.

The law allows:
- State universities, community colleges, ISDs, and school districts
  to authorize charter schools
- For-profit management companies to operate charter schools
- A 3% authorization fee (incentivizing authorizers to keep schools open)
- No statutory authorizer evaluation system
- Board members appointed without identity disclosure requirements

**Source:** [Founders Library](https://charterlibrary.org/timeline/michigan/);
[MI Good Government](https://migoodgovernment.com/2025/03/using-compromise-to-pass-charter-school-legislation/);
Dykema.com Education page

**Actors:** Richard McLellan, Leonard Wolfe (Dykema)

---

### Jan 14, 1994 — Engler Signs PA 362

Governor Engler signs the charter school law. Michigan becomes the
9th state to enact a charter school law.

**Source:** [Founders Library](https://charterlibrary.org/timeline/michigan/)

**Actors:** John Engler, Richard McLellan, Leonard Wolfe

---

### 1994 — First Charter Schools Open

Central Michigan University becomes the first authorizer. Three
schools open: Casa Maria Academy (Detroit), New Branches School
(Grand Rapids), Northlane Math and Science Academy.

**Source:** [CMU Center for Charter Schools](https://www.thecenterforcharters.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/15Year.pdf)

---

### 1997 — Dykema Defends the Law at Supreme Court

The Michigan Education Association challenges the constitutionality
of PA 362. Dykema's McLellan and Wolfe **defend the law** before the
Michigan Supreme Court in *Council of Organizations and Affiliates v.
Engler*, 455 Mich 557 (1997).

The Court **upholds the law** and orders state funding restored.

Court record lists: *"Dykema, Gossett, P.L.L.C. by Richard D.
McLellan, Lori M. Silsbury, and Leonard C. Wolfe, Lansing, for
intervening Defendants-Appellants."*

**Source:** [Justia](https://law.justia.com/cases/michigan/supreme-court/1997/106092-1.html);
455 Mich 557

**Actors:** Richard McLellan, Leonard Wolfe, Lori Silsbury (Dykema)
