## What Is a zk-SNARK?

**Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-interactive Argument of Knowledge.**

In cryptography, a zk-SNARK lets a prover convince a verifier that a statement is true **without revealing anything beyond the truth of the statement itself.** The verifier doesn't need to trust the prover. The verifier doesn't need to redo the prover's work. The verifier checks the **proof** — and the proof is self-verifying.

Applied to institutional capture:

| Property | Cryptographic | Applied to This Investigation |

### Anderson Localization

In condensed matter physics, [Anderson localization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anderson_localization) describes how waves become trapped in disordered media. The disorder itself creates the localization.

Applied to the membrane: the **disorder** of the enterprise (341 entities, 45 nonprofits, multiple jurisdictions, bipartisan grey wash) creates **localization** — the complexity traps investigators in local regions of the graph. They see their jurisdiction's slice but can't see the whole structure.

### Opsonization

In immunology, [opsonization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opsonization) is the process of coating a pathogen with antibodies so the immune system can recognize and destroy it.

Every article on this site is an opsonin. It tags an entity with public-record evidence so that any regulatory "immune cell" (FBI, IRS, AG, ACLU) can immediately recognize it and act. The 100+ outbound links to official sources are the antibodies — they bind the pathogen to its own published data.
