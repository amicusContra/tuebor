He Fed the Sheep to the Wolves

A 9-time convicted felon ran charter schools in Detroit. The church blessed it. The congregation was never told.

The Shepherd

The Church of God in Christ (COGIC) is the largest Black Pentecostal denomination in America. 6.5 million members. Founded in Memphis in 1897 by Bishop Charles H. Mason — son of formerly enslaved parents — who built a church from nothing.

Bishop Mason’s legacy was a sanctuary.

The Presiding Bishop endorsed Brian Banks.

The Wolf

Brian Banks. Nine criminal convictions. Ran Purpose Christian Academy (PCA) — a charter school in Detroit. Public money flowed in. Purpose Group LLC siphoned it out. The school paid rent to a building Banks controlled. The school paid management fees to a company Banks owned. The children paid with their futures.

3% math proficiency. Three percent. In a school that received public education dollars. In a city where parents trusted the church to protect their children.

The endorsement told the congregation: this man is safe. The public record says: this man has nine convictions.

Nobody told the parents.

The Gate

How does a convicted felon get endorsed by the largest Black Pentecostal denomination in America?

The same way he gets authorized to run a charter school. The same way he gets approved by the state. The same way he survives for 14 years.

Nobody checks.

Or: everybody checks, and nobody says anything. The silence IS the gate.

The authorization chain:

  1. Central Michigan University authorized the charter
  2. Michigan Department of Education provided oversight (theoretically)
  3. COGIC leadership endorsed the operator
  4. Dykema Gossett PLLC wrote Michigan’s charter law in 1993, then represented charter operators, then funded the politicians who kept it deregulated
  5. Walton Family Foundation funded the journalism that normalized charters, the fellowship that credentialed operators, and the lobby that blocked accountability

Every link in the chain had a choice. Every link chose silence.
