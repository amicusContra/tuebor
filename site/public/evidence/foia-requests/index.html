TCR 22-12 — Final Decision and Order (August 4, 2023)

State Superintendent Michael F. Rice issued Banks’s administrator certificate with seven (7) restrictive conditions:

ConditionRequirement
#1Background check every 3 years
#2Report criminal charges to MDE within 3 business days
#3Shall not handle cash
#4Shall not be the sole person to approve expenditures
#5Complete 6 credit hours professional development annually
#6Submit to compliance verification every 6 months
#7Board verifies compliance every 6 months (signed by Board Chair)

Key details the FOIA revealed:

  • The Department of Education objected to reinstatement
  • The Attorney General argued against the certificate
  • The Administrative Law Judge recommended no conditions (rubber stamp)
  • Superintendent Rice overruled the ALJ and added the 7 conditions
  • The compliance verifications under Condition #7 are signed by Nicole Wells-Stallworth — MacDowell Board Chair and former CEO of The Children’s Center of Wayne County

The note that matters: David Head signaled that compliance verification documents exist but were not part of the original request. This prompted FOIA Request #2.
