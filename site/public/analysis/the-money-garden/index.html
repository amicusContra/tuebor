The Law

It all started with one law.

PA 362 — Michigan Charter School Law (1993). Written with the involvement of Dykema Gossett.

Dykema wrote the law. DeVos lobbied for it. The law created charter schools. The schools created Purpose Charter Academy. PCA created the extraction pipe to Purpose Group LLC. And the money flowed.

The Number
