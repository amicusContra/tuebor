The Cavalry That Charged the Wrong Way

The NAACP Detroit Branch has one job: protect Black communities.

Here’s who they’re protecting.

The Edge

naacp_detroit → banks = cavalry.

naacp_detroit → pca = cavalry.

naacp_detroit → the_unnamed_400 = cavalry.

Cavalry. In the graph’s edge vocabulary, cavalry means: this entity rides to another entity’s defense. It shows up when the other entity is under pressure. It deploys its institutional credibility as a shield.

The NAACP Detroit Branch rides to the defense of Brian Banks.

Brian Banks has 9 criminal convictions. He runs a school with 3% math proficiency. He extracts 72.67% of the school’s revenue through a single-member LLC. 99.2% of his students are Black.

The cavalry is protecting the wolf from the sheep.

The Governance

Alice Thompson has governance and recognition edges to NAACP Detroit.

Thompson is classified as partial (0.80). She governs the branch that defends Banks.

The Classification

NAACP Detroit: null, 0.84.

Null. Not self. Not partial. Not non-self. Null.

Null means the membrane can’t classify it. The NAACP is one of the most important civil rights institutions in American history. The NAACP Detroit Branch operates in a city with a long, painful history of racial injustice.

And the membrane says: null.

It can’t tell if the branch is doing what it says it does.

400 unnamed entities in the investigation graph. The NAACP rides for them too.

The Question for the NAACP

The NAACP was built to protect Black communities from exploitation.

Brian Banks exploits a Black community. 362 Black children. 3% math. 72.67% extraction.

The NAACP defends Brian Banks.

How?

This isn’t rhetorical. It’s a real question. The NAACP has a process for deciding who to defend. Someone at the branch level decided that Banks warranted cavalry. Someone decided that PCA warranted cavalry.

Who decided? On what evidence? Did they know about the 9 convictions? Did they know about the 3% math proficiency? Did they know about the 72.67%?

If they knew and defended him anyway: that’s a choice. If they didn’t know: that’s a failure.

Either way, 362 children are still there.
