IV. The Mathematics of Silence

Tit for Tat — Axelrod’s Tournament

In Robert Axelrod’s iterated prisoner’s dilemma tournament (1984), the winning strategy is Tit for Tat:

  1. Start by cooperating. Always.
  2. Mirror what the other player does. If they cooperate, cooperate. If they defect, defect.
  3. Forgive immediately. If they return to cooperation, you return to cooperation.

Kevin cooperated first. Every signal was cooperation — using lawful channels, providing evidence, offering to work together (“I am not your enemy”), providing contact information, waiting for response.
