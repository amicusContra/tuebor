What the Law Says

FERPA (20 U.S.C. §1232g): Custodial parents have the right to access their children’s education records and information about their children at school.

MCL 380.1137: Schools shall not deny a parent access to their child except by court order.

MCL 600.916: It is illegal to represent yourself as an attorney if you are not one. Banks did this on August 29 to justify denying access. DPD Report #26-0909-0176 documents it.
