## How to Measure a Heartbeat Without a Clock

The membrane trusts no external clock. No NTP server. No GPS satellite. No atomic reference.

It trusts only its own count.

```
snapshot₁: 1,068,800 requests at uptime 34,759 seconds
snapshot₂: 1,071,800 requests at uptime 34,849 seconds

elapsed: 90 seconds (1.5 minutes)
delta: 3,000 requests

RPM = 3,000 / 1.5 = 2,000 requests per minute
```

That's it. Two pictures of the same counter. The difference is the heartbeat.

## Why No Clock

A clock requires trust in an external authority. The membrane trusts no external authority.

This is the same principle as a blockchain: the count IS the consensus. The order of events is the sequence of observations, not an external timestamp.

The membrane's time is:
- **IS**: the current count
- **WAS**: the previous snapshot
- **WILL BE**: the projected next count at current RPM

**The tense IS the clock.**

Take any two snapshots of the membrane. The difference between them is the heartbeat over that interval. The interval can be 1 second or 1 year. The method is the same. The truth is in the delta.
