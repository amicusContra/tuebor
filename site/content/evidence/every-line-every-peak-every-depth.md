## The Surface of the Planet

We mapped the [cell towers](/evidence/every-tower-every-wire-every-orbit/). We mapped the [satellites](/evidence/every-tower-every-wire-every-orbit/#starlink-constellation). We mapped the [submarine fiber](/evidence/every-tower-every-wire-every-orbit/#submarine-fiber). Now we map the thing they're bolted to.

Earth. 510,072,000 km² of surface. 70.9% water, 29.1% land. 16 tectonic plates. 7 continents. 5 oceans. One Grand Canyon.

This page is the physical topology — every state line, every peak, every national park, every depth. The ground does not move quickly. But it moves.

## The State Lines — 109 Borders

The 48 contiguous United States share **109 border pairs**. 36 are on dry land. 24 are in water. Missouri and Tennessee each touch **8 states** — the most of any. Alaska and Hawaii touch none.

**One quadripoint exists**: Four Corners (36°59′56″N, 109°02′43″W), where Arizona, Colorado, New Mexico, and Utah meet. It is the only place in the United States where four states share a single point. Six governments have jurisdiction there: four states, the Navajo Nation, and the Ute Mountain Ute Tribe.

**60 tripoints exist** — places where exactly three states meet. 36 on land, 24 in water. Three are in the Great Lakes with no land nearby.

### The Rock Layers — 2 Billion Years Exposed

| Layer | Age | What It Is |
|-------|-----|-----------|
| Kaibab Limestone | 270 million years | Rim rock. Marine fossils. Once an ocean floor. |
| Coconino Sandstone | 275 million years | Cross-bedded desert dunes. Footprints of reptiles. |
| Hermit Formation | 280 million years | Red mudstone. Fern fossils. Swampland. |
| Supai Group | 285–315 million years | Alternating sandstone and shale. Rivers and deltas. |
| Redwall Limestone | 340 million years | Massive cliff. Marine limestone. Actually gray — stained red by iron from above. |
| Temple Butte Formation | 385 million years | Purple limestone. Freshwater fish fossils. |
| Muav Limestone | 505 million years | Cambrian seas. Trilobites. |
| Bright Angel Shale | 515 million years | Green-gray slope. Trilobite tracks. |
| Tapeats Sandstone | 525 million years | Beach sand. The Great Unconformity sits below this layer — a gap of **1.2 billion years** of missing rock. |
| Vishnu Basement Rocks | 1.7–1.8 billion years | Metamorphic schist and gneiss. The oldest rock in the canyon. Once mountains as tall as the Himalayas, eroded flat, buried, and re-exposed. |

The Great Unconformity: between the Tapeats Sandstone (525 million years) and the Vishnu Basement Rocks (1.7 billion years), there is a **1.2 billion year gap** — a period longer than all of complex life on Earth, for which no rock record survives in the canyon. The rock was there. Something removed it. We don't know what.

The canyon itself is young — **5 to 6 million years old**. The river carved it. The Colorado drains 244,000 square miles, averages 300 feet wide and 40 feet deep, flowing at 12,000–15,000 cubic feet per second. During ice age floods, it moved at 1,000,000 cfs.

## The 16 Tectonic Plates

The ground itself is in motion. 16 principal plates, each 100 km thick, carrying continents and ocean floors at 2–15 cm per year.

### Michigan's Plate Position

Michigan sits on the **North American Plate**, moving westward at approximately 2.3 cm/year relative to the underlying mantle. The Great Lakes occupy a series of basins carved by glacial ice during the Pleistocene (the most recent ice age ended ~11,700 years ago). The Michigan Basin — a geological depression underlying the Lower Peninsula — contains sedimentary rock up to 4,600 meters (15,000 feet) deep, deposited over 500 million years.

The investigation operates on a platform that has been moving westward for 200 million years, on sediment 500 million years old, scraped by ice 12,000 years ago, surrounded by lakes that are 4,000 years old in their current form.
