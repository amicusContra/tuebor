## The Narrative Cycle

This is not a conspiracy. It's an *operating system*.

**Step 1: Fund the narrative.**
Walton pays for Roland Martin's "School Choice Is the Black
Choice" tour. Ten cities. Partnered with The 74 — a news site
that Walton also funds, founded by an advocate who sits on the
board of the DeVos-chaired school choice lobby. The message:
charter schools = Black empowerment. Choice = freedom.

**Step 2: Fund the credential pipeline.**
Walton pays for Pahara Institute fellowships. Pahara makes Brian
Banks — a man with 9 criminal convictions — into a "Pahara Fellow."
The fellowship is designed to "strengthen values-driven leaders."
Banks's bio now reads like a redemption narrative:
*"Having personally navigated the very systems that continue to fail
many Detroit youth."*

What it doesn't say: he defrauded a credit union, violated campaign
finance laws, and runs a school where 3% of students are proficient
in math while 72.67% of revenue flows to his single-member LLC.

**Step 3: Fund the lobby.**
Walton pays MAPSA $4.3M+ to fight charter regulation in Michigan.
Michigan is already the most deregulated charter state in America.
Public Act 277 (2011) lifted the charter cap entirely. 80% of
Michigan charters are run by for-profit companies. The result: a
$1B/year industry with the weakest accountability in the nation.

**Step 4: Shield with "Jim Crow 2.0."**
Roland Martin — now a trusted voice in Black media — frames any
attack on Black institutions as "Jim Crow 2.0." The framing is
real and valid when applied to voter suppression, redistricting,
and defunding. But it creates an immune response that also shields
charter extraction from scrutiny. Criticize a Black-led charter
school? You're attacking Black agency. Question the money flow?
You're questioning Black entrepreneurship.

**Step 5: Celebrate at the dinner.**
Roland Martin is honored at the Detroit NAACP Freedom Fund Dinner.
10,000 attendees. Alongside Alice Thompson — a Black charter
network leader whose organization received a Skillman Foundation
planning grant. The credential is now circular: the narrative
legitimized the schools, the schools legitimized the leaders,
the leaders are honored by civil rights organizations, and the
honor legitimizes the narrative.
