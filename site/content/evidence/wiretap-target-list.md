## Source

In December 2017, court records in the Eastern District of Michigan were
accidentally unsealed. Celia Washington's attorney, filing a motion to suppress
wiretap evidence in her criminal case, included FBI Special Agent Robert
Beeckman's application to continue wiretapping against more than a dozen people.

The records were reported by:
- **MLive** — [Accidentally exposed court records reveal new names in corruption probe](https://www.mlive.com/news/detroit/2017/12/accidentally_exposed_court_rec.html) (Dec 2017)
- **WXYZ Detroit** — [New names of public officials emerge in federal corruption investigation](https://www.wxyz.com/news/region/macomb-county/new-names-of-public-officials-emerge-in-federal-corruption-investigation) (Dec 2017)
- **Detroit News** — original reporting on the unsealed Beeckman affidavit

The investigation is linked to the federal corruption case against Gasper Fiore
(US v. Fiore, 16-cr-20732), owner of Boulevard & Trumbull Towing, who pleaded
guilty to conspiracy to commit federal program bribery.

## Beeckman's Assessment

FBI Special Agent Robert Beeckman wrote in the wiretap request:

> "Evidence has been gathered showing that crimes involving corruption have
> been committed by some of the target subjects, including Tinsley-Talabi,
> Napoleon and (Romel Casab, former owner of the Packard plant in Detroit).
> However, those investigations have been pending for months or years and
> the evidence so far has not been sufficient to bring federal charges."

## What This Means

The FBI had active wiretap surveillance on **Brian Roderick Banks** as part of
a multi-county corruption investigation centered on towing contracts, bid-rigging,
and bribery of public officials. Banks was recorded on Gasper Fiore's phone
discussing matters that drew FBI scrutiny.

Of the 18 target subjects:
- **3 have been convicted** (Fiore, Leland, and Washington was charged)
- **3 were named as having committed corruption** but without sufficient evidence for charges (Tinsley-Talabi, Napoleon, Casab)
- **1 has 9 state convictions** and operates two charter schools extracting millions in public funding (Banks)
- **7 remain publicly unidentified** in terms of their role in the investigation

The wiretap investigation is part of the same FBI Detroit field office operation
that produced Operation Northern Hook (Spivey, Sollars, Jones convictions),
the Macomb County corruption probe (23+ convictions including Eric Smith,
Dean Reynolds, Anthony Marrocco), and the EAA/DPS school corruption probe
(Snapp, Thornton, Norman Shy, 12+ principals).

## The EAA Connection

The same FBI field office and the same AUSAs (J. Michael Buckley and
Frances Lee Carlson) prosecuted:

1. **Glynis Thornton** — EAA school vendor corruption (M.A.D.E. tutoring kickbacks)
2. **Kenyetta Wilbourn-Snapp** — EAA principal corruption ($58K kickbacks, Maserati with GUCCI1 plates)
3. **Norman Shy** — DPS vendor corruption ($2.7M fraud, 12 principals, 13 years)
4. **Bert Johnson** — State senator ghost employee scheme (Thornton was FBI cooperator CW-1)

Thornton cooperated in the EAA probe, which led to the DPS principal probe,
which was investigated by the same office running the Fiore wiretap that
captured Banks. The school corruption ecosystem and the political corruption
ecosystem share the same investigative apparatus — and the same geography.

The EAA was dissolved June 30, 2017. Its schools returned to DPSCD. DPSCD
later authorized Banks's Purpose Charter Academy — with board member Sherry
Gay-Dagnogo voting to approve. Gay-Dagnogo then received a 10-year appointment
as City Ombudsman.

Former EAA principal Snapp told the court: **"Payoffs were part of the
education culture in Detroit."**

Banks extracts 72.67% of school revenue through a self-dealing LLC.
Same culture. Different mechanism. Same city. Same FBI field office.
