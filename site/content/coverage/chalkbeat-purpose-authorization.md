## The Article

**[Detroit school district approves new charter school for struggling youth](https://www.chalkbeat.org/detroit/2025/09/08/detroit-school-district-plans-to-authorize-a-new-charter-school/)** — Chalkbeat Detroit, September 8, 2025

Chalkbeat reported that DPSCD authorized Purpose Charter Academy to serve students
experiencing homelessness, chronic absenteeism, and juvenile justice involvement.
The school would lease a vacant DPSCD building with an option to purchase.
DPSCD receives a 3% authorization fee (~$81,000/year).

## The Oversight Gap

Chalkbeat quoted board member Sherry Gay-Dagnogo supporting the authorization.
This evidence library documents [Gay-Dagnogo's campaign finance connections](/network/political/sherry-gay-dagnogo/) to the same network.

Michigan law (MCL 380.507) requires closure of schools in the bottom 5% for three
consecutive years. Michigan law (MCL 380.502) allows suspension of an authorizer's
power for inadequate oversight. Neither has been triggered.
