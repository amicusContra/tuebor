## The Article

**[FOIA and Your Michigan School Board](https://clutchjustice.com/2026/09/23/foia-michigan-school-board-records-guide/)** — Clutch Justice (Rita Williams), September 23, 2026

A practical legal guide for parents who want to obtain records from their child's
charter school board using Michigan's Freedom of Information Act (MCL 15.231).

## Why This Matters for This Case

This evidence library was built almost entirely from publicly accessible databases.
But some records — board meeting minutes, internal financial documents, management
agreements — require FOIA requests. Clutch's guide gives parents the legal framework
to obtain the same types of records that revealed MacDowell's gift-card payments
and operating deficit.
