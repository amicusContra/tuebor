## How to Look Up Campaign Finance Records

Every political candidate, PAC, and committee in Michigan must file campaign finance reports. These reports show who gives money, who gets money, and how much.

The filings are public. The search is free.

---

## Step 1: Go to the Secretary of State

Michigan campaign finance records are maintained by the **Secretary of State.**

Visit: [michigan.gov/sos](https://www.michigan.gov/sos) → Elections → Campaign Finance

Or search directly through the **Campaign Finance Reporting** system.

---

## Step 2: Search

You can search by:
- **Candidate name** (e.g., "Yancey" or "Miller")
- **Committee name** (e.g., "Save Detroit Jobs")
- **Contributor name** (e.g., "Banks Strategy")
- **Filing period** (by year or election cycle)

---

## Step 3: Read the Filings

### Expenditure Reports
Shows where campaign money goes. This is where you'd find:
- Yancey's payments to **Banks Strategy & Consultants LLC**
- Yancey's payments to **Inner Link LLC**
- Any payments to entities in the Banks network

### Contribution Reports
Shows where campaign money comes from. Look for:
- Contributions from Banks-connected individuals
- Contributions from Banks-connected LLCs
- Bundled contributions from the same network

### PAC Reports
PACs (Political Action Committees) file separately. Look for:
- **Save Detroit Jobs** — connected to Dykema, supported Latisha Johnson
- **Dykema State PAC** — Dykema's political arm
- Any PAC with edges to charter school entities

---

## What to Look For

| Red Flag | What It Means |
|----------|-------------|
| Judge paying campaign funds to a non-attorney's LLC | Potential violation of judicial conduct |
| PAC created shortly before election supporting one candidate | Dark money targeting |
| Multiple payments to LLCs owned by same person | Dispersed payments to avoid attention |
| Campaign paying for "consulting" from convicted person | Campaign funds flowing to extractive entity |

For federal candidates and PACs:
- **FEC.gov**: [fec.gov](https://www.fec.gov/) → Campaign Finance Data

- **TransparencyUSA**: [transparencyusa.org](https://www.transparencyusa.org/)
