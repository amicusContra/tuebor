The Board

Here's who sits on it.

The Fiduciary Problem

Board members have a fiduciary duty to the school and its students.

The board approves the management contract with Purpose Group LLC — the contract that sends 72.67% of revenue to Banks.

  • Moreland represents Banks AND approves the contract that pays Banks
  • Miller chairs the board AND has campaign donation edges to Banks
  • Ison works for the AG AND serves on the board the AG should be examining
  • Johnson was elected via infrastructure connected to Dykema AND governs the school Dykema's law enabled

Every member has a conflict. Every conflict points the same direction: toward Banks.

What a Clean Board Looks Like

A clean charter school board would have:

  • No members who represent the school's founder
  • No members who work for the office investigating the founder
  • No sitting judges
  • No members elected via PACs connected to the school's legal infrastructure
  • Independent financial oversight
  • Background checks on board candidates

PCA's board has none of these safeguards.
