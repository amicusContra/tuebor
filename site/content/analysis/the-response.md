## What the Law Actually Says

The PPO was filed under **MCL 600.2950a** (nondomestic personal protection orders) citing **MCL 750.411h** and **MCL 750.411i** (stalking statutes).

Michigan's stalking statute (MCL 750.411h) defines stalking as:

> *"A willful course of conduct involving repeated or continuing harassment of another individual that would cause a reasonable person to feel terrorized, frightened, intimidated, threatened, harassed, or molested."*

Sending documented evidence of public corruption through lawful channels to public institutions is not harassment. It is:

- **FOIA requests** — protected under Michigan Freedom of Information Act (MCL 15.231)
- **Complaints to regulatory agencies** — protected activity
- **Publication of public records** — protected under the First Amendment
- **Petitioning the government** — protected under the First Amendment (Petition Clause)

Michigan does not have a comprehensive anti-SLAPP statute, but the First Amendment applies regardless.
