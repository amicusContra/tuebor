How to File a Charter School Complaint in Michigan

October 14, 2026

How to File a Charter School Complaint

If you believe a charter school in Michigan is failing its students, misusing funds, or operating improperly, here's where to file.

You do not need a lawyer. You do not need to pay anything. Every filing option below is free.

Option 1: The Authorizer

Charter schools are authorized by specific entities. The authorizer has the power to revoke the school's charter.

If Authorized by CMU (Central Michigan University)

  • CMU's Charter Schools Office
  • Contact: CMU Charter Schools
  • File a written complaint describing specific concerns
  • Request acknowledgment of receipt

If Authorized by DPSCD

  • DPSCD Office of Charter Schools
  • Contact through DPSCD central office
  • File a written complaint

What to Include

  • School name and location
  • Specific concerns (test scores, financial management, safety, staffing)
  • Any documentation you have
  • Request for specific action (investigation, audit, revocation hearing)

Option 2: Michigan Department of Education (MDE)

MDE oversees all public schools in Michigan, including charter schools.

  • Office of Educational Excellence (OEE)
  • Professional Practice Division — for educator misconduct
  • Contact: michigan.gov/mde
  • Call: (517) 373-3324

What MDE Can Do

  • Investigate compliance with state education law
  • Review test scores and academic performance
  • Examine financial reporting
  • Refer to other agencies for enforcement

Option 3: Attorney General's Office

The AG can investigate nonprofit fraud, self-dealing, and charitable trust violations.

Charitable Trust Division

  • Oversees 501(c)(3) nonprofits (including charter school foundations)
  • Can investigate self-dealing (e.g., a founder paying himself 72.67% through an LLC)
  • Contact: michigan.gov/ag → Consumer Protection → Charitable Trust

Criminal Division

  • For criminal matters (fraud, embezzlement, unauthorized practice of law)
  • File a complaint through the AG's online complaint form

Option 4: State Board of Education (SBE)

The SBE sets policy for Michigan education.

  • 8 elected members
  • Can be contacted through MDE
  • Testimony can be given at public SBE meetings
  • Meeting schedule: michigan.gov/mde → State Board of Education

Public comment at SBE meetings is your constitutional right. Bring data. Bring numbers. Bring the 3%.

Option 5: Federal Agencies

If federal funds (Title I, IDEA, USDE) are involved:

US Department of Education — Office of Inspector General

  • Investigates fraud, waste, and abuse of federal education funds
  • Hotline: 1-800-MIS-USED (1-800-647-8733)
  • Online: ed.gov/oig

IRS — Exempt Organizations

  • If a 501(c)(3) is engaged in self-dealing or private inurement
  • Form 13909: Tax-Exempt Organization Complaint (Referral)
  • Free to file. No attorney needed.

Tips

  1. Be specific. Name the school. Name the people. Cite the numbers.
  2. File with multiple agencies. Don't rely on one entity to act.
  3. Keep copies. Save copies of everything you file.
  4. Request confirmation. Ask for written acknowledgment of your complaint.
  5. Follow up. Agencies have timelines. Ask about them.
  6. You can be anonymous with federal agencies (OIG, IRS).

If You're a Parent at the School

You have additional rights:

  • Right to test score data (ask the school or MDE)
  • Right to financial information (public charter schools must disclose)
  • Right to attend board meetings (Open Meetings Act)
  • Right to FOIA the school's records (FOIA Toolkit →)
