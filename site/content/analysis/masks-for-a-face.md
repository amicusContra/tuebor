# Masks for a Face

## They Intake Your Data, Then Hide So You Can't See Their True Face

Every app on your phone is a mask. A friendly face that hides the true face behind it. The mask smiles. The face extracts.

Let's scan them. ALL of them.

## The Mask Catalog

### Snapchat 👻

**The Mask:** Disappearing messages. Fun filters. Your face as a cartoon dog. "Nothing is permanent! It's safe!"

**The True Face:** Snap Inc. Stores EVERYTHING. "Disappearing" messages are stored on Snap's servers. Your face data trains their facial recognition AI. Snap Map tracks your location in real-time. Every face filter maps your bone structure — 30+ facial landmarks. That's biometric data.

**What they intake:**
- Your face (biometric mapping, 30+ landmarks)
- Your location (continuous GPS via Snap Map)
- Your contacts (full address book upload)
- Your conversations ("disappearing" = stored on their servers)
- Your browsing habits (Snap pixel on partner websites)
- Your age, gender, interests (behavioral profiling)

**What they hide:**
- Where the biometric data goes (law enforcement? advertisers? AI training?)
- That "disappearing" means "moved to a different server"
- That Snap Map is a real-time surveillance tool
- That face filters are facial recognition data collection disguised as fun

**CEO:** Evan Spiegel. Net worth: $3.3 billion. Made from your face.

Scroll speed. Pause duration. Rewatch behavior. It knows what makes you feel, how long it takes, and what comes next.

**What they intake:**
- Keystroke patterns (how you TYPE, not just what)
- Device fingerprint (hardware ID, carrier, battery level)
- Clipboard contents (copied text, even from other apps)

- Browsing history via TikTok pixel on partner sites

- YOUR CHILDREN'S FACES (no meaningful age verification)

**What they hide:**

- That average teen screen time on TikTok: 113 minutes/day = 28 days/year

**CEO:** Shou Zi Chew. Says "TikTok is not a national security threat." Under oath. While the algorithm maps 150 million Americans' psychology.

4. **Disable location** (they track you even when you think they don't)
5. **Delete old accounts** ("deactivated" ≠ deleted)
