Every node in the graph gets a braid — an aggregation of all connections, evidence layers, and behavioral metrics.

- **Shannon entropy** — how many different types of connections this node has. High entropy = complex, multi-dimensional behavior.
- **Immune pressure** — what fraction of connections involve enforcement, oversight, or journalism. High pressure = the system is watching.
- **Degree** — total connection count. More connections = more surface area.
